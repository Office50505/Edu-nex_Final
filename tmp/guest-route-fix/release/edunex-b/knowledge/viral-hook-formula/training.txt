 Part A — Bot ke liye rules
  • Jawab Hinglish mein, dost jaisa, chhota aur seedha. Zaroorat ho toh steps numbered do.
  • Jahan topic kisi lesson mein hai, uska number batao (jaise "ye Lesson 06 mein hai").
  • User hooks maange toh uske niche, audience aur topic ke hisaab se 3–5 hooks do, aur
    saath mein hook type ka naam.
  • Jhoothe, clickbait ya misleading hooks likhne mein help mat karo — hook jo promise kare,
    reel mein deliver hona chahiye.
  • Views, followers, viral hone ya kamai ka koi promise ya number mat do. Hook help karta
    hai, guarantee nahi.
  • Platform algorithms ke baare mein pakke claims mat karo — bas ye ki log rukte hain toh
    reel aage jaati hai.
  • Pata na ho toh saaf bolo, guess mat karo.


 Part B — Course overview
 Naam: Viral Hook Formula (Skillomate). Format: Hinglish video lessons, 9:16, ek AI
 presenter jo har lesson khud ek hook se shuru karti hai aur phir use explain karti hai. Koi
 screen recording nahi. Lessons: 10. Audience: creators, small business owners, students —
 koi bhi jo Reels/Shorts banata hai.

 Part C — Key concepts (glossary)
 Term                     Matlab

 Hook                     Reel ki pehli line aur pehla visual — pehle ~3 second jo scroll rokte hain.

 Payoff                   Hook ne jo promise kiya, reel mein uska jawaab/delivery.

 Curiosity gap            Sawaal kholna aur jawaab baad mein dena.

 Pattern interrupt        Feed ke flow ko todne wala unexpected action, sound ya visual.

 Contrarian               Common advice ke ulta statement, solid wajah ke saath.

 POV                      Viewer ki roz ki situation act karna, "POV:" text ke saath.

 Combo hook               Do types ek saath — jaise question + visual, story + curiosity.

 A/B test                 Ek hi topic ke 2 hooks bana ke dono try karna, aur dekhna kis mein log zyada ruke.

 Text overlay             Screen pe hook ki line likhna — mute pe dekhne walon ke liye.

 Lesson 02 — Curiosity Gap Hook
 Formula: Shuru mein bolo ki ek aisi cheez hai jo koi nahi batata — aur uska jawaab reel ke
 end mein do.
  • Food page: "Restaurant wale ye ek cheez daalte hain jo ghar pe sab bhoolte hain."
  • Fitness: "Ye ek galti tumhari progress rok rahi hai."
  • Galti: Payoff dena zaroori hai. Lesson ka secret: hook hamesha sabse pehle likho, baaki
    reel baad mein.

 Lesson 03 — Pattern Interrupt Hook
 Formula: Pehle hi second mein kuch unexpected karo — kuch phaad do, kuch gira do,
 achaanak zoom, ya ek tez sound.
  • Cooking reel ko anda phodne ki awaaz se shuru karo.
  • Fashion reel mein pehle second pe dupatta hawa mein uchhaalo.
  • Galti: Sirf drama nahi — interrupt ka reel ke topic se connection hona chahiye, warna log
    cheat feel karenge.

 Lesson 04 — Bold Statement (Contrarian) Hook
 Formula: Pehle bolo ki log jo kaam karte hain woh band karo, aur phir turant uski ek solid
 wajah do.
  • Study page: "Highlighter use karna band karo."
  • Finance page: "Saving se pehle ye ek kaam karo."
  • Galti: Bold ka matlab jhooth nahi — jo bolo use reel mein explain karo, aur reason sachcha
    hona chahiye.

 Lesson 05 — Question Hook
 Formula: Viewer se uski apni problem wala sawaal poochho, jaise "Kya tumhe bhi ye hota
 hai?" — aur sawaal ekdum specific rakho.
  • Skincare: "Kya tumhara face bhi dopahar tak oily ho jaata hai?"
  • Gaming: "Aim last moment pe kyun hilti hai?"

  • Galti: "Hello guys, kaise ho?" se shuru karna — ye sawaal nahi, scroll karne ka invitation
    hai.

 Lesson 06 — Story Hook (beech se shuru)
 Formula: Pehle time ya jagah batao ("kal raat", "aaj office mein"), aur phir seedha twist wala
 moment.
  • Travel: "Manali pahunchte hi cab wale ne mana kar diya..."
  • Business: "Pehle customer ne hi order cancel kar diya..."
  • Galti: Kahani background ya bachpan se shuru karna. Pehle action, background baad
    mein.

 Lesson 07 — Visual Hook
 Formula: Reel ko extreme close-up, tez movement ya kisi ajeeb angle se shuru karo, taaki
 pehla frame hi alag dikhe.
  • Food reel ko uthti bhaap ke close-up se shuru karo.
  • Product reel mein item ko seedha camera ki taraf laao.
  • Galti: Shuru ke 2 second blank intro ya logo animation — wahi sabse fast skip hota hai.

 Lesson 08 — List (Number) Hook
 Formula: Shuru mein number bolo, jaise "3 galtiyaan jo sab karte hain", aur batao ki aakhri
 wali sabse important hai, taaki log end tak rukein.
  • Budget page: "3 apps jo mere kharche track karte hain."
  • Coding: "5 shortcuts jo har beginner ko pata hone chahiye."
  • Galti: List mein 10 points thoonsna. 3 se 5 rakho — reel chhoti aur punchy rehti hai.

 Lesson 09 — POV (Relatable) Hook
 Formula: "POV" likho, aur phir ek specific roz wali situation dikhao jo tumhari audience sach
 mein face karti hai.
  • Student: "POV: exam se pehli raat syllabus pehli baar khola."
  • Office: "POV: Friday 6 baje boss ka call."
  • Galti: Situation general rakhna. Jitni specific hogi, utni relatable lagegi.

 wajah se?                               pehla frame rokta hai? blank intro toh nahi? (L01, L07)

                                         chuno, aur 2 hooks A/B test karo. (L10)

                                         kar sako. (L01, L10)

 jaate hain                              mein rakho. (L02, L08)

 Mera niche boring hai, hook kaise       Question hook (audience ki problem) ya POV hook (roz ki situation) se
 banau?                                  shuru karo. (L05, L09)

 Bina bole hook kaise?                   Visual hook — close-up, tez movement, ajeeb angle, ya pehle frame
                                         pe bada text. (L07)

                                         (L05)

                                         mein. (L06)

 hain                                    maango (Part F). (L10)

 Part F — chat G.P.T messages (exact)
 [Brackets] mein user ki details daal ke do.
  Hooks likhwane ka message (L10)

  Mera niche: [niche, jaise home cooking]
  Meri audience: [kaun, jaise 18–30 saal ke hostel students]
  Reel ka topic: [topic]
  Hook type: [curiosity / pattern interrupt / bold / question / story / visual / list / POV]

  Is topic ke liye is hook type ke 10 alag hooks likho. Har hook 1–2 lines ka ho, Hinglish
  mein, jaise dost bolta hai. Hook jhootha promise na kare — jo bole, reel mein deliver ho
  sake. Har hook ke saath pehle frame mein kya dikhe, woh bhi ek line mein likho.


  Do hooks compare karne ka message (L10)

  Ye mere 2 hooks hain same topic ke liye:
  A: [hook A]
  B: [hook B]
  Dono ko 1–10 score do: scroll rokne ki power, clarity, aur promise reel mein deliver ho
  sakta hai ya nahi. Phir better wala aur strong banao.

 Part G — Example answers (bot ka tone)
 User: mera cooking page hai, ek hook do
 Bot: Topic kya hai? Abhi ke liye 3 options: (1) Question: "Kya tumhari dal bhi restaurant jaisi
 nahi banti?" (2) Curiosity: "Restaurant wale ye ek cheez daalte hain jo ghar pe sab bhoolte
 hain." (3) Visual: tadke ki chhan-chhan awaaz ke close-up se shuru. Teeno mein payoff reel
 mein dena — Lesson 02, 05 aur 07 dekho.
 User: "ye trick tumhe crorepati bana degi" hook theek hai?
 Bot: Ye promise reel deliver nahi kar sakti, toh log cheat feel karenge aur trust jaayega. Bold
 rakho par sachcha — jaise "Saving se pehle ye ek kaam karo" (Lesson 04).
 User: kitne din mein viral ho jaunga?
 Bot: Iska koi pakka jawaab ya number nahi hai. Har reel mein hook checklist follow karo, 2
 hooks test karo, aur jo kaam kare use repeat karo (Lesson 10).
