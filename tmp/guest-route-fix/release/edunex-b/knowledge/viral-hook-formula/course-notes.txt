Lesson 01 — Pehle 3 Second Ka Khel (Intro)
Lesson ka hook: "Ruko! Scroll mat karna — ye 3 seconds tumhari reel ki kismat tay karte
hain." Aur phir: "Yahi maine abhi tumhare saath kiya — tum ruk gaye. Ise kehte hain hook."
Kya hai: Hook matlab reel ki pehli line aur pehla visual — jo scroll karte thumb ko rok de.
Kyun kaam karta hai: Instagram aur YouTube pehle yahi dekhte hain ki log ruke ya nahi.
Log ruke, toh reel aage jaati hai.
Formula: Course ka format: har lesson mein presenter khud ek naya hook try karti hai, phir
use tod ke samjhaati hai. 8 types: curiosity, pattern interrupt, bold statement, question,
story, visual, list, POV.
Galti / rule
Hook jhootha nahi hona chahiye — jo promise karo, reel mein woh dena hi padega.

Lesson 02 — Curiosity Gap Hook
Lesson ka hook: "Ek cheez jo koi bhi creator tumhe nahi batata... woh is lesson ke end mein
bataungi. Toh stay karo!"
Kya hai: Dimaag mein ek sawaal kholo, aur jawaab thoda baad mein do.
Kyun kaam karta hai: Dimaag ko adhoori cheez bilkul pasand nahi. Sawaal khula hai, toh
banda jawaab tak rukega.
Formula: Shuru mein bolo ki ek aisi cheez hai jo koi nahi batata — aur uska jawaab reel ke
end mein do.
Examples
•Food page: "Restaurant wale ye ek cheez daalte hain jo ghar pe sab bhoolte hain."
•Fitness: "Ye ek galti tumhari progress rok rahi hai."
Galti / rule
Payoff dena zaroori hai. Lesson ka secret: hook hamesha sabse pehle likho, baaki reel baad
mein.
Aaj ka kaam
Apne niche ke liye 5 curiosity hooks likho.
Lesson 03 — Pattern Interrupt Hook
Lesson ka hook: Presenter pehle second mein kaagaz phaad deti hai: "Ye tha tumhara
purana content plan. Ab naya plan banate hain."
Kya hai: Kuch aisa jo feed mein baaki sab se alag dikhe ya sunaayi de.
Kyun kaam karta hai: Feed mein sab ek jaisa chalta hai. Achaanak kuch alag hua, toh
aankh khud ruk jaati hai.
Formula: Pehle hi second mein kuch unexpected karo — kuch phaad do, kuch gira do,
achaanak zoom, ya ek tez sound.
Examples
•Cooking reel ko anda phodne ki awaaz se shuru karo.
•Fashion reel mein pehle second pe dupatta hawa mein uchhaalo.
Galti / rule
Sirf drama nahi — interrupt ka reel ke topic se connection hona chahiye, warna log cheat feel
karenge.
Aaj ka kaam
Apni agli reel ka ek unexpected pehla second plan karo.

Lesson 04 — Bold Statement (Contrarian) Hook
Lesson ka hook: "Daily post karna band karo." Phir: "Haan, sahi suna. Quality wali 3 reels,
bekaar wali 7 se better hain."
Kya hai: Woh bolo jo common advice ke bilkul ulta ho.
Kyun kaam karta hai: Koi ulta bolta hai, toh dimaag turant poochhta hai — "arre, kyun?" —
aur wahin ruk jaata hai.
Formula: Pehle bolo ki log jo kaam karte hain woh band karo, aur phir turant uski ek solid
wajah do.
Examples
•Study page: "Highlighter use karna band karo."
•Finance page: "Saving se pehle ye ek kaam karo."
Galti / rule
Bold ka matlab jhooth nahi — jo bolo use reel mein explain karo, aur reason sachcha hona
chahiye.
Aaj ka kaam
Apne niche ki ek common advice pakdo aur uska ulta hook likho.
Lesson 05 — Question Hook
Lesson ka hook: "Tumhari reel log 2 second mein skip kyun kar dete hain? Kyunki pehla
sawaal hi boring tha."
Kya hai: Viewer se seedha woh sawaal poochho jo uske dimaag mein pehle se ghoom raha
hai.
Kyun kaam karta hai: Sawaal sunte hi dimaag jawaab dhoondhne lagta hai — aur banda
automatically engage ho jaata hai.
Formula: Viewer se uski apni problem wala sawaal poochho, jaise "Kya tumhe bhi ye hota
hai?" — aur sawaal ekdum specific rakho.
Examples
•Skincare: "Kya tumhara face bhi dopahar tak oily ho jaata hai?"
•Gaming: "Aim last moment pe kyun hilti hai?"
Galti / rule
"Hello guys, kaise ho?" se shuru karna — ye sawaal nahi, scroll karne ka invitation hai.
Aaj ka kaam
Apni audience ki 3 problems likho, aur har ek ko sawaal wala hook banao.
Lesson 06 — Story Hook (beech se shuru)
Lesson ka hook: "Kal raat 2 baje mere phone pe ek message aaya... aur uske baad maine
apna poora content style badal diya."

Kya hai: Kahani shuru se nahi, sabse interesting moment se shuru karo.
Kyun kaam karta hai: Kahani mein dimaag khud ko daal deta hai. Aur kahani beech mein
ho, toh end jaanna zaroori lagta hai.
Formula: Pehle time ya jagah batao ("kal raat", "aaj office mein"), aur phir seedha twist wala
moment.
Examples
•Travel: "Manali pahunchte hi cab wale ne mana kar diya..."
•Business: "Pehle customer ne hi order cancel kar diya..."
Galti / rule
Kahani background ya bachpan se shuru karna. Pehle action, background baad mein.
Aaj ka kaam
Apni life ka ek moment lo, aur use beech se shuru karke hook likho.
Lesson 07 — Visual Hook
Lesson ka hook: Presenter ek laal apple seedha camera ki taraf laati hai: "Pehle aankh ruki,
phir kaan khule — hai na?"
Kya hai: Pehla frame itna alag ho ki bina sound ke bhi rukna pade.
Kyun kaam karta hai: Bahut log reels mute pe scroll karte hain. Isliye pehla frame hi asli
hook hai.
Formula: Reel ko extreme close-up, tez movement ya kisi ajeeb angle se shuru karo, taaki
pehla frame hi alag dikhe.
Examples
•Food reel ko uthti bhaap ke close-up se shuru karo.
•Product reel mein item ko seedha camera ki taraf laao.
Galti / rule
Shuru ke 2 second blank intro ya logo animation — wahi sabse fast skip hota hai.
Aaj ka kaam
Apni agli reel ka pehla frame mute karke dekho — kya woh akele rokta hai?
Lesson 08 — List (Number) Hook
Lesson ka hook: "3 hooks jo sabse zyada kaam karte hain... question, story aur list. Aur aaj
seekhenge list wala."
Kya hai: Number sunte hi bande ko pata chal jaata hai ki kitna value milega.
Kyun kaam karta hai: Number dimaag ko clear promise deta hai — chhoti list, fast value,
aur end tak rukne ki wajah.
Formula: Shuru mein number bolo, jaise "3 galtiyaan jo sab karte hain", aur batao ki aakhri
wali sabse important hai, taaki log end tak rukein.

Examples
•Budget page: "3 apps jo mere kharche track karte hain."
•Coding: "5 shortcuts jo har beginner ko pata hone chahiye."
Galti / rule
List mein 10 points thoonsna. 3 se 5 rakho — reel chhoti aur punchy rehti hai.
Aaj ka kaam
Ek list wali reel plan karo, aur sabse strong point aakhir mein rakho.
Lesson 09 — POV (Relatable) Hook
Lesson ka hook: "POV: tumne 4 ghante edit kiya, aur reel pe aaye 200 views." Phir:
"Relatable laga na?"
Kya hai: Viewer ki apni roz ki situation act karo, taaki woh soche — "arre, ye toh main hoon!"
Kyun kaam karta hai: Jab banda khud ko reel mein dekhta hai, toh woh rukta bhi hai aur
doston ko send bhi karta hai.
Formula: "POV" likho, aur phir ek specific roz wali situation dikhao jo tumhari audience sach
mein face karti hai.
Examples
•Student: "POV: exam se pehli raat syllabus pehli baar khola."
•Office: "POV: Friday 6 baje boss ka call."
Galti / rule
Situation general rakhna. Jitni specific hogi, utni relatable lagegi.
Aaj ka kaam
Apni audience ke 5 roz wale moments likho, aur har ek ko POV hook banao.
Lesson 10 — Finale: Apna Hook Formula
Lesson ka hook: "Ye woh aakhri cheez hai jo tumhe hooks ke baare mein jaanni hai... par
sirf tab, jab tum end tak ruko." (Curiosity + bold ek saath.)
Kya hai: Recap: curiosity, pattern interrupt, bold statement, question, story, visual, list aur
POV — 8 hooks.
Kyun kaam karta hai: Best hooks aksar do types mila ke bante hain — jaise question +
visual, ya story + curiosity.
Formula: chat G.P.T se hooks likhwao: apna niche, audience aur hook type batao, aur bolo
"10 options do". Phir ek hi topic ke 2 hooks banao, dono try karo, aur dekho kis mein log
zyada ruke.
Galti / rule
Golden rule: hook ek promise hai. Reel mein woh promise poora karo, warna trust chala
jaayega.

Hook checklist (post karne se pehle)
•Pehli line 3 second ke andar khatam ho jaati hai?
•Mute pe bhi pehla frame rokta hai?
•Pehle 2 second mein koi blank intro ya logo nahi?
•Hook specific hai, general nahi?
•Jo promise kiya, reel mein deliver ho raha hai?
•Text overlay pe hook ki line likhi hai (mute viewers ke liye)?
