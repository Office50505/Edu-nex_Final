 MODULE 1 — FOUNDATION
Lesson 1 — Introduction
Kya hai: AI influencer ek digital model hai jo bina camera, bina model, bina sharam aur bina kharche ke
tumhare liye din-raat content banati hai. Ek hi phone se tum ek poori "creator + business" chala sakte ho.
Ye 3 kaam karti hai:
 • Content — daily reels aur photos (Instagram/YouTube).
 • Business — kisi brand/product ke professional photos aur videos, ya kisi brand ka "face" banna.
 • Paisa — followers se sponsorship, ads, affiliate, aur apna product bech ke.
Kyun ye ab possible hai: AI tools itne saste + aasan ho gaye ki na coding, na editing degree, na English
chahiye. Sirf seekhne ki niyat.
Common galti: log sochte hain "ye mushkil/technical hai" — actually ye copy-paste level aasan hai jab process
pata ho.
Aaj ka kaam: decide karo tumhe content banana hai, business karna hai, ya paisa — teeno ka rasta isi course
mein hai.

Common galti: alag-alag accounts se sign-up karke files/history kho dena.
Aaj ka kaam: teeno tools install + ek hi Google account + "AI Studio" folder.

Lesson 3 — AI Influencer ka Character Banana
Sabse bada concept = CONSISTENCY. Face har photo mein bilkul same hona chahiye — warna audience
aur brands trust nahi karte (log ek pehchana chehra follow karte hain).
Character decide karo (kahin likh lo): naam, age, city, personality, interest — jaise "Neha, 22, Mumbai,
fashion + travel". Ye persona uske content ka flavour decide karta.
Face banane ka tarika: ek detailed prompt se face generate karo, 4 nikaalo, sabse achhi + real wali ko
REFERENCE PHOTO ke roop mein save karo. Ye ek photo aage har kaam (pose, outfit, location, video) mein
lagegi.

Common galti: har baar naya face banana (consistency toot jaati). Ek hi reference pakdo.
Aaj ka kaam: character details likho + face generate karo + best ko reference save.
■ PROMPT (face banane ke liye):
A young Indian woman, early 20s, oval face, warm brown eyes, long dark wavy hair, soft friendly
smile, natural clear skin, wearing a casual top, standing in soft natural daylight, realistic ca
ndid photo, shot on iphone. 9:16.

Lesson 4 — Prompt Likhna Seekho
Pure course ki sabse zaroori skill — achha prompt = achhi photo, bura prompt = bekaar output.
Prompt ke 5 hisse (order mein):
 1. Kaun — age, gender, nationality ("a young Indian woman, early 20s").
 2. Face details — aankh, baal, skin, smile ("oval face, brown eyes, wavy hair").
 3. Kapde — outfit specific ("white cotton kurti").
 4. Jagah/background — location + mahaul ("cafe, soft window light").
 5. Style — realistic / candid / shot on iphone.
Rules: English mein likho (weak ho to ChatGPT se translate). "Beautiful/gorgeous" jaise adjectives bekaar —
details do. Ek prompt mein 2-3 main cheez, zyada maangoge to AI confuse.
Common galti: vague prompt ("ek sundar ladki") → random output.
Aaj ka kaam: 5 prompts likho (base same, sirf jagah + kapde badalke).
■ PROMPT (structure):
use the exact same face, same woman, same facial features, [outfit], [location + mood], realisti
c photo, shot on iphone, natural light

 MODULE 2 — PHOTO SKILLS
Lesson 5 — Same Face, Alag Pose
Concept: reference photo + magic line "Use this exact face. Same woman, same facial features." → chehra
same rehta, pose/angle/jagah badal jaati.
Zaroori:
 • Hamesha original reference se banao. Nayi banayi photo ko dobara reference mat banao — warna face
 dheere-dheere badalta jayega (copy ki copy = drift).
 • AI 100% match nahi deta har baar — 4 generate karo, best 1 rakho.
Common galti: nayi photo ko reference bana ke chain continue karna → face slowly badal jaata.
Aaj ka kaam: reference se 5 alag photos (jagah/kapde/pose), best save.
■ PROMPT:
use the exact same face, same woman, same facial features, now she is [new pose / angle / sittin
g / walking], realistic photo, shot on iphone

Lesson 6 — Outfits Badalna
4 style ka wardrobe banao (zyada styles = zyada clients/brands):
 • Casual (daily/relatable), Ethnic (Indian/festival), Western (premium/office), Party wear (occasion).
Tips: outfit ke saath lighting match karo (party → evening/warm, casual → natural day). Ek photo mein main
kapda + 1 accessory + lighting — bas; zyada maangoge to AI gadbad karta.
Common galti: ek hi style ki photos (limited kaam milta).
Aaj ka kaam: chaar style ki ek-ek photo.

■ PROMPT:
use the exact same face, same woman, same facial features, now she is wearing [specific outfit],
 [matching lighting], realistic photo, shot on iphone

Lesson 7 — Locations Badalna
5 location type: Ghar (relatable), Cafe (aesthetic), Street (fashion), Nature (travel), Premium (hotel/rooftop —
luxury/brands).
Tips: sirf jagah nahi, mahaul likho ("sunset in background, wind in hair"). Location ke hisaab se pose bhi badlo
(candid > stiff). Famous monument mat maango (AI bigaad deta) — "historic Indian architecture" jais■ general.
Common galti: har jagah same khadi pose (boring + AI lagta).
Aaj ka kaam: paanch location type ki photos.
■ PROMPT:
use the exact same face, same woman, same facial features, now she is standing on a Goa beach at
 sunset in a flowy summer dress, wind in hair, sunset in background, candid pose, realistic phot
o, shot on iphone

Lesson 8 — Realistic Skin & Details (AI-look hatao)
Plastic/AI-look hatane ke liye prompt mein 4 cheez:
 1. natural skin texture, visible pores
 2. slight imperfections, few flyaway hairs
 3. shot on iPhone / DSLR
 4. soft natural light
Aur "candid" word add karo — natural pose + real-friend-snap feel.
Ulta kaam karne wale words: "ultra realistic, 8K, HD, perfect" — ye actually zyada AI/plastic bana dete. Inhe
avoid karo.
Common galti: "8K ultra realistic" likhke sochna real aayega — ulta hota hai.
Aaj ka kaam: 3 purani photos ko in 4 details ke saath dobara banao, side-by-side compare.
■ PROMPT:
use the exact same face, same facial features, now she is sitting in a cafe wearing a white shir
t, candid natural moment, shot on iphone, soft natural light, natural skin texture, visible pore
s, slight imperfections

Lesson 9 — Photo Quality Fix
Save karne se pehle checklist:
 1. Haath/ungliyan — 5 hain? mudi to nahi?
 2. Aankhein — dono ek jagah, same direction?
 3. Background — kuch bigda/melt to nahi?
 4. Face match — reference se milta hai?
Repair kaise: "Fix only the hands/eyes... keep everything else exactly the same" (word only zaroori). Ya
kharab hissa crop karo. Ya aisi pose banao jisme haath chhup jaye (pocket/bag/crossed).
Rule: ek photo pe max ek fix — do problem ho to delete karke nayi banao (fix pe fix quality girata).
Common galti: ek hi photo ko baar-baar fix karna → aur bigadti.
Aaj ka kaam: saari photos checklist pe check; fail wali fix ya delete.
■ PROMPT:
fix only her hand in this image, fingers natural and correctly shaped, five fingers, relaxed pos
e, keep the face hairstyle outfit pose lighting and background exactly the same, do not change a

nything else

Lesson 10 — 30 Photos ka Content Bank
Batch banao: ek hi din mein poore mahine ka content — 30 photos = 5 outfits × 6 locations. Regular posting
hi Instagram ka rule, aur roz banana practical nahi.
Kaise: ChatGPT se 30 numbered prompts likhwao (saari details already daalke), phir Flow mein ek-ek chala
ke generate.
Reality: 30 banao to 20-22 achhi milengi (normal AI hit-rate). Har 5 mein 1 candid rakho (feed real lage).
Common galti: roz ek-ek photo banana (thak jaoge, consistency tootegi). Batch = smart.
Aaj ka kaam: 30 prompts list + kam se kam 10 photos aaj (baaki 2 din mein).
■ PROMPT:
generate multiple photos of her, same exact face: 1 morning coffee at home, 2 working on laptop
in cafe, 3 evening street walk, 4 reading at night, 5 rooftop golden hour... (numbered, har mein
 outfit+location details), realistic, shot on iphone

 MODULE 3 — VIDEO & REELS
Lesson 11 — Photo se Video Banana
Kyun: Reels = Instagram ka asli game — photo se 10× reach, aur brands video ke zyada paise dete.
Kaise: photo upload → prompt mein chhoti movement likho (smile, blink, coffee sip, hair sway) + "natural
motion". Video ~8 sec (reels ke liye kaafi).
Rule: movement chhoti rakho — badi harkat (chalna, ghoomna) mein face bigadta. 2-3 try, best rakho.
Common galti: badi/complex movement maangna → face melt/warp.
Aaj ka kaam: 3 best photos se video (chhoti movements).
■ PROMPT:
[attach photo] She smiles softly, blinks naturally, slowly lifts the coffee cup and takes a sip,
 gentle camera movement, natural motion. 9:16.

Lesson 12 — Lip-Sync Video (bolti Neha)
2 step: pehle script (jo bolegi), phir Flow mein bulwana.
Prompt: "She looks at the camera and speaks naturally... saying: [words]".
Rules: line chhoti (8-10 sec, 15-20 words). Hindi word galat bole to sound ke hisaab se spelling likho
("zindagi" → "zindagee"). Ek video = ek baat.
Reality: lip-sync mushkil hai — 3-4 try normal.
Common galti: lamb■ line/paragraph → mouth out of sync, cut off.
Aaj ka kaam: ek intro line (15-20 words) + lip-sync video, best save.
■ PROMPT:
[attach photo] Real handheld iPhone video. She looks at the camera and speaks naturally, friendl
y expression, saying: "Hi guys! Mumbai se hu main. Fashion aur travel meri zindagee hai. Naye ho
 toh follow karlo!" 9:16.

Lesson 13 — AI Voice Banana
Concept: awaaz bhi face jaisi consistent honi chahiye. Voice ke 3 hisse: pitch (soft/deep), speed (pace), tone
(friendly/calm).

Kaise: prompt mein voice line add karo aur har video mein bilkul same likho (reference line ki tarah). Fancy
words ("melodious/honey voice") mat — simple napi-tuli details.
Pro tip: agar Flow ki awaaz AI-ish lage, edit mein apni recording ya ElevenLabs Hindi voice overlay karke
Flow-voice mute kar do.
Common galti: har video mein alag voice description → awaaz badalti rehti.
Aaj ka kaam: voice description final + notes mein save + ek video us line se.
■ PROMPT (voice line):
...she speaks naturally, soft warm female voice, young Indian accent, medium pace, saying: "[lin
e]"

Lesson 14 — Reel Editing Basics (VN)
VN mein sirf 4 kaam:
 1. Ratio 9:16 (full-screen reel).
 2. Cut/jodna — pehla aadha second kaato (turant action se shuru = watch-time).
 3. Captions — jo bol rahi wahi text (white + black outline). 80% log bina awaaz dekhte.
 4. Music — volume 20-30% (awaaz hero rahe).
Export 1080p. Transitions/effects ke chakkar mat pado — simple hi professional.
Common galti: over-editing (zoom, effects) → cheap/cluttered lagta.
Aaj ka kaam: pehli poori reel (4 steps), export.
*(VN skill — koi generation prompt nahi.)*

Lesson 15 — 5 Reel Formats
5 formats (idea kabhi khatam nahi):
 1. GRWM (get ready with me), 2. Day in Life, 3. Tips (3 value tips), 4. Trends (trending audio = free reach),
 5. Product (sabse keemti — brands ke liye).
Rule: har format har baar viral nahi — rotate karo (hafte 3 reels, 3 alag formats). 20-30 reels baad pattern
samajh aata (kya chalta).
Common galti: ek format pe atakna, ya viral ka intezaar. Consistency > perfection.
Aaj ka kaam: 2 formats chuno, dono ki 1-1 reel.
■ PROMPT (batch photo + animate):
generate 4 photos of her (same face): morning coffee / laptop work / evening walk / reading at n
ight
now animate all 4: 1 sips coffee slowly, 2 types and smiles, 3 gentle wind as she walks, 4 turns
 a page softly

Lesson 16 — Profile Setup (professional)
Kyun: log reel dekh ke 3 sec mein profile pe decide karte hain follow kare ya nahi; brands profile 10 baar check
karte.
4 cheez:
 1. Username — chhota, naam se juda, no numbers/underscores.
 2. Profile photo — close-up face (full body nahi — chhoti screen pe pehchana jaaye).

 3. Bio (3 line) — kaun + kahan se + hook, 1-2 emoji.
 4. Professional/Creator account — free analytics (kaun■■ reel chali, followers kab active).
Common galti: naye account pe 10 posts ek saath thok dena → spam shak, reach dabti.
Aaj ka kaam: profile taiyaar + ek achhi pehli post.

Lesson 17 — Posting Schedule + Captions & Hashtags
Schedule: algorithm regularity dekhta. Hafte 3 reels (3 formats), fixed din/time (jaise Mon-Wed-Fri raat 8 —
followers active time analytics se).
Caption formula: hook (pehli line) → 1-2 line value/story → call-to-action.
Hashtags: 10-15 mix (bade + medium + chhote niche), 2-3 sets bana ke rotate.
Common galti: caption mein "nice day ■" — caption post ka salesman hai, use karo.
Aaj ka kaam: weekly schedule + 3 caption templates + 3 hashtag sets.

Lesson 18 — Reels Algorithm Samjho
Soch: algorithm dushman nahi, dukaandaar — usko log app pe rukwane hain. Jo reel rokti hai, wo aage
badhti.
Reel ka safar: pehle ~200 logon ko dikhti → achha response → 2000 → aur aage.
3 sabse important: (1) watch time — pehla second sabse zaroori, (2) completion/re-watch — loop banao,
(3) shares & saves — likes se zyada powerful.
Pehla 1 ghanta: active time pe post + comments ka turant reply.
Common galti: boring pehla second → log scroll → reach mar jaati.
Aaj ka kaam: apni best reel ka pehla 2 sec pattern dekho aur repeat karo.

Lesson 19 — 0→1000 Followers + Engagement
1000 kyun khaas: app serious leta (reach khulti) + brands ki duniya (nano-influencer 1000-10000 brands ke
sabse pasandeeda — engagement zyada, rate sasta).
Roadmap: 1000 pahaad nahi — hafte 3 reels/3 formats → 2 mahine = 24 reels → 2-3 chalengi → 1000. Shuru
ke followers apne circle se (koi sharam nahi).
Engagement (followers rokna + jodna): like/comment/reply/share. Engaged account = zinda account, sirf
number se zyada keemti.
Roz ka 20-min routine: (1) comments ka reply (khaas pehle ghante), (2) story mein poll/sawaal/BTS, (3)
doosron ke posts pe 10-15 genuine comment (niche follow, spam nahi).
Common galti: post karke gayab — Instagram do-tarfa hai, jo judta wahi badhta.
Aaj ka kaam: 8-hafte reel plan + aaj ka 20-min routine.

Lesson 21 — Professional Studio Shots
Saste vs mehnge ka farak = 3 cheez:
 1. Lighting — soft studio light, hard shadow nahi.
 2. Background — plain white/grey + product ke neeche soft shadow ("rakha hua" lage, chipka nahi).
 3. Angle — bottle/jar eye-level, jewellery/watch upar se close, shoes side.
Har product ke 2-3 angle/variation do (choice = professional).
Common galti: flat lighting + no shadow → product "floating"/fake lagta.
Aaj ka kaam: ek product ko studio-level — soft light + clean bg + 3 angles (catalog set).
■ PROMPT:
Product photo of [product], professional studio setup, soft key light, plain white/grey backgrou
nd with a soft shadow under it, [eye-level / top / side] angle, premium commercial look, ultra r
ealistic, 4K

Lesson 22 — Lifestyle + Platform-Ready Shots
Lifestyle: product ke saath zindagi (watch kalai pe cafe mein, candle jalti cozy room). Log feeling khareedte —
brands iske zyada paise dete.
Platform-ready:
 • Amazon/Flipkart main image = plain white bg, product bada, no text.
 • Instagram/website = lifestyle + square/vertical crop.
Sahi size/crop mein do (client ko dobara crop na karna pade = professional).
Common galti: ek hi photo har jagah bhejna — pehle poochho "photo kahan use hogi".
Aaj ka kaam: ek lifestyle photo + ek Amazon-ready white photo (ek set).
■ PROMPT:
[product] held in hand in a real [cafe/desk/outdoor] setting, candid lifestyle, shot on iphone,
natural light, realistic || + separate: [product] on plain white background, product large a
nd centered, no text, catalog style, 4K

Lesson 24 — Pehla Client Kaise Laye (Outreach)
Mindset: tum salesman nahi, ek problem ka hal de rahe (saste professional photos jo sales badhati).

 MODULE 6 — UGC & BRAND DEALS
