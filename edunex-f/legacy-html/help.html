Support

How can we help?

Use these support paths for account access, videos, payments, courses, and profile issues.

Login Or Profile

If your login, OTP, avatar, or profile details do not sync, log out and sign in again. Then open Profile.

Course Videos

Video access requires an active trial or subscription. Open Courses, then choose View Course.

Payments

After payment, continue on web to return to courses. Subscription status can be checked from the Profile page.

Wishlist

Tap the heart on a course card to save it. Logged-in accounts sync wishlist data when the backend is available.

Nex AI

Use Nex AI inside the video player for summaries, study plans, project ideas, and lesson explanations.

Contact

Email support at support@edunex.ai with your mobile number and issue details.
