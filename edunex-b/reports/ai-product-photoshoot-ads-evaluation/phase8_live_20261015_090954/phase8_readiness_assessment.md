## What the run establishes

Fresh 100-case execution through actual routing, retrieval, prompt assembly, real configured fal.run interaction when needed, session-state persistence calls and response guards. All 119 user-facing turns are captured. Deterministic local outputs, actual live-model text and two validation replacements are distinguished. The 50 new cases were authored after v5 freeze and before responses, with unfamiliar products, all three languages, multi-turn, reasoning, safety and exact prompts. They were not ingested into application knowledge.

Provider access succeeded (13 scored requests plus one preflight, all first-attempt HTTP 200). This sample does not establish sustained uptime, tail latency under load or every fallback pathway. Existing local fallback tests pass; no natural outage occurred.

## Blocking findings

Known names remain unresolved in two complete approved templates and one requested template is replaced by general workflow. These are critical fidelity blockers even though frozen v5 misses two. New paraphrases also expose FAQ visibility, colour-vs-description, language-replacement, final-workflow, compound-task and saved-context defects. These require separate authorized remediation and local validation, followed by another frozen fresh evaluation. No repair is applied in this run.

V5 also needs a separately reviewed revision: nested product phrases, regenerate equivalents, Hindi prohibition scope, and deliverable-level comparison checks. The evaluator is preserved unchanged here; acceptance criteria must not be softened merely to meet a target.

## Important limits

An HTTP application pipeline with synthetic auth/account/course/entitlement and in-memory session fixtures is not production authentication/database validation. The catalogue fixture contains the product course only; real multi-course persistence/isolation and production load require their own evidence. Only approved course-specific content was sent: non-course platform/payment and synthetic learner context were projected out after actual prompt assembly.

Agent manual review is not independent external-human sign-off. Finite language/safety/concept rules have both false failures and false passes. The unchanged Phase 6 historical 95/100 v5 regrade remains historical and is not a Phase 7 live score. Phase 8 uses a different 50/50 composition, so cross-suite headline scores are not a controlled improvement estimate.

No deploy, commit, push, production-resource change, application edit, evaluator edit or post-freeze test change occurred. Stop here for review.
