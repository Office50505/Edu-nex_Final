 Part A — Bot ke liye rules
    • Jawab Hinglish mein, dost jaisa, chhota aur seedha. Zaroorat ho toh steps numbered do.
    • Jahan kaam kisi lesson mein hai, uska number batao (jaise "ye Lesson 03 mein hai").
    • Prompt maange toh course ka exact ChatGPT message do (Part F), user ki property details
    daal ke.
    • Honesty sabse upar: kabhi room ka structure badalne, nakli view/amenity dikhane, ya bina
     'Virtually Staged' label ke staged visual dene mein help mat karo.
    • AI visuals ko asli shoot ya asli property footage bata kar dikhane mein help mat karo.
    • Kamai, clients, sales ya views ka koi promise ya number mat do. Service ka price user
    khud decide karta hai.
    • Local real-estate advertising rules jagah ke hisaab se alag hote hain — user ko apne area
    ke current rules check karne ko bolo.
    • Tools ki price, credits ya limits ke pakke number mat batao.
    • Course ke tools: ChatGPT, Google Flow (Nano Banana Pro + Video), CapCut. Bahar ke tools
    course ka part nahi.
    • Pata na ho toh saaf bolo, guess mat karo.

 Part C — Key concepts (glossary)

  Term              Matlab

   Listing brief           Property ke selling points, sahi buyer aur reel ka mood — sirf asli details se.

  Shot list              Reel ke har shot ka room, kya dikhana, camera move, mood aur seconds.

  Room Lock          Har room ki ek English line — walls, windows, doors, flooring, ceiling, size, camera
                       angle — jo AI kabhi nahi badlega. Har prompt mein jaati hai.

   Virtual staging         Khaali room ki photo mein AI se furniture/decor add karna — structure same.

   Virtually Staged       Disclosure label jo har AI-staged photo/video par dikhna chahiye.

   Light upgrade           Sirf lighting aur time of day badalna (golden hour, evening) — view aur building same.

  Frames mode        Flow Video ka mode jisme start frame (aur chahe toh end frame) se video banta hai.

   Start / end frame     Video ki pehli / aakhri image. Day-to-evening mein dono lagte hain.

  FPV                Drone jaisa tez, smooth camera move jo andar ya upar se guzarta hai.

  Montage            Chhote fast shots ko order mein jodkar bana trailer.

  Scene (Flow)         Flow mein clips ko order mein jodne ki jagah — 'Add clip' se.


 Part D — Workflows (short)

 Lesson 02 — Property Ka Plan (Brief + Shot List + Room Lock)
    • Course ki sample flat ki 7 photos (exterior, living, 2 bedroom, kitchen, bathroom, balcony)
  — inhe 'sample flat' hi bolna hai.
    • ChatGPT ko property ki asli details do: BHK, floor, location, size, highlights, buyer. Usse
     listing brief milta hai — selling points, sahi buyer, reel ka mood. Koi fake feature nahi.
    • Shot list: har shot ka room, kya dikhana hai, camera move, mood, seconds. Exterior se
     shuru, balcony pe end.
    • Room Lock: saari photos upload karke har room ki ek English line — walls, windows, doors,
     flooring, ceiling, size, camera angle — jo AI kabhi nahi badlega. Ye is course ka 'product
     sheet' hai, har aage ke prompt mein jaata hai.
    •  Jis photo ki asli photo nahi hai (jaise parking/lift), uska shot skip karo — invent mat karo.

 Lesson 03 — Virtual Staging
    • Staging prompt bhi ChatGPT se: pehli line 'Use the attached photo as the exact room. Only
    add furniture and decor — do not change anything else.' + Room Lock line + style.
    • Flow → Image → Nano Banana Pro, khaali photo reference mein, prompt paste, generate.
    • Before/after compare karo: window, flooring, camera angle same — sirf furniture naya.
    • Bedroom bhi same tareeke se. Doosra style (Scandinavian) bhi same Room Lock ke saath
  — client ko options.
    • Galti: Sirf 'Furnish this room nicely' likhne se AI ne room ka structure hi badal diya — ye
     listing mein dhokha hai.

 Lesson 04 — Light Upgrade (Day se Golden Evening)
    • ChatGPT se 3 prompts: exterior golden hour, living room evening (lamps on), balcony
    sunset — pehli line 'Only change the lighting and time of day'.

    • Har prompt mein Room Lock + 'bahar ka view, buildings, trees, furniture same rahe — koi
    naya view/pool/garden nahi'.
    • Living room ke liye L03 wali furnished photo use karo, khaali wali nahi.
    • Rule: bahar ka view kabhi naya mat banao — jo asli hai wahi dikhao.
    • Galti: 'make it look beautiful at night' likhne se night toh bani, par saamne ki road, cars,
    aas-paas sab badal gaya.

 Lesson 05 — Walkthrough Video
    • Phone se khaali flat ka din wala video koi bhi bana lega — AI ka fayda: furnished, evening,
    day-to-evening jo shoot ho hi nahi sakta.
    • Flow → Video → Frames mode, 9:16, 8 sec. Start frame = photo.
    • Har prompt: 'Only the camera moves' + ek simple slow move (push-in, glide, pan).
    • Day-to-evening: start frame din wala living room, end frame evening wala, camera static —
     lights dheere on hoti hain.
    • Galti: 'camera flies through the whole house fast' — AI ne naye kamre aur corridor bana
    diye jo flat mein hain hi nahi.

 Lesson 06 — Cinematic Montage (Fast Shots)
    • Slow walkthrough boring — buyer ko fast, cinematic trailer chahiye.
    • Shots jo phone se nahi bante: FPV drone rise, floor-level rapid push-in, macro pull-back,
    whip-pan half-arc, FPV balcony push.
    • Har clip 4 sec, sirf EK fast move — taaki room morph na ho. Room Lock + 'no new rooms,
     objects, views or amenities'.
    • Paanchon ek saath generate karo. Phir Flow mein clip kholke scene banao, 'Add clip' se
    baaki shots order mein jodo: exterior → andar → balcony.
    • Pehla ChatGPT jawab slow lage toh poora naya message do jisme speed aur angles saaf
     likhe hon.

 Lesson 07 — Final Listing Reel
    • ChatGPT se reel ka text: on-screen lines, 'Virtually Staged' disclaimer, caption + hashtags,
    music mood — sirf brief wali details, price ki jagah 'Price on request'.
    • CapCut: clips import, 9:16 timeline, montage pehle, phir day-to-evening clip.
    • End card par saaf, bada disclaimer: 'Virtually Staged — furniture and decor are digitally
    added for visualization.'
    • Export 1080p, 9:16 — reel ready, client ko bhejo.

 Lesson 08 — Service Banana (Finale)
    • ChatGPT se 3 packages (Basic / Standard / Premium) — kya deliver hoga; price tum khud
    decide karo. Har package mein disclosure included.
    • Client checklist: kaunsi photos/angles, asli details, aur AI staging + disclosure ki permission
  — kaam se pehle lo.
    • Broker ko pehla WhatsApp message: simple, friendly, sample reel ke saath — koi 'zyada
     sales' ya 'guaranteed buyers' jaisa promise nahi.
    • 5 honesty rules: clear disclosure, original structure, no fake views, no fake amenities,
    honest representation.

 Part F — Saare ChatGPT messages (exact)

  User ki property ki details daal ke do. Saare messages ek hi chat mein bhejne hain.

  Lesson 02 · Message 1: Property brief


   Main ek property ke liye listing reel banana chahta hoon. Details:
   - Property: 2BHK flat, 3rd floor
   - Location: Vijay Nagar, Indore
   - Size: approx 1100 sq ft
   - Highlights: east-facing balcony, modular kitchen, achhi sunlight, society mein parking
   aur lift
   - Buyer: young working couple ya chhoti family


   Is property ka ek short listing brief banao — main selling points, kis buyer ke liye best
   hai, aur reel ka mood kya hona chahiye. Koi feature apni taraf se mat jodna, sirf jo maine
   bataya.



  Lesson 02 · Message 2: Shot list


   Ab is property ki 30–40 second ki vertical listing reel ka shot list banao. Har shot ke
   liye table do: shot number, room, kya dikhana hai, camera move (jaise slow push-in, side
   glide), mood/lighting, aur kitne seconds. Exterior se shuru karo aur balcony pe end karo.
   Har shot 8 second ya kam ka ho.

  Lesson 02 · Message 3: Room Lock (saari photos upload karke)


   Ye is property ki photos hain. Har photo ke liye ek "Room Lock" line likho — us room ki
   woh cheezein jo AI editing mein KABHI change nahi honi chahiye: walls, windows ki position
   aur size, doors, flooring, ceiling, room ka size aur camera angle. English mein, ek-ek
   line, taaki main ise har image prompt mein paste kar sakun.



  Lesson 03 · Galti wala prompt (Flow, sirf comparison ke liye)


   Furnish this room nicely



  Lesson 03 · Message 1: Staging prompts (ChatGPT)


   Ab virtual staging karni hai. Living room aur master bedroom ke liye Google Flow (Nano
   Banana Pro) ka ek-ek image prompt likho. Main khaali room ki photo reference mein upload
   karunga.


   Har prompt mein:
   - Pehli line: "Use the attached photo as the exact room. Only add furniture and decor — do
   not change anything else."
   - Us room ki Room Lock line jo tumne pehle likhi thi, word-to-word
   - Style: modern Indian home — warm wood, beige aur off-white fabrics, ek indoor plant,
   soft warm lighting
   - Furniture ka size room ke hisaab se realistic ho, room bada ya chhota na dikhe
   - Same camera angle, same phone-photo look, no people, no text


   Dono prompts English mein, copy-paste ready.



  Lesson 03 · Message 2: Scandinavian style (ChatGPT)


   Ab living room ka ek aur prompt do, same Room Lock ke saath, par style alag — minimal
   Scandinavian: light oak wood, white aur grey, clean simple furniture. Baaki sab rules
   same.



  Lesson 04 · Galti wala prompt (Flow, sirf comparison ke liye)


   make it look beautiful at night

  Lesson 04 · Message 1: Light upgrade prompts (ChatGPT)


   Ab light upgrade karna hai. Google Flow (Nano Banana Pro) ke liye 3 image prompts likho,
   main har baar wali photo reference mein upload karunga:


   1. Building exterior — golden hour evening: warm sunset light, soft orange-pink sky,
   balconies aur windows ki lights on
   2. Staged living room (modern Indian wala) — evening look: lamps aur ceiling lights on,
   warm cozy light, window ke bahar halki sham
   3. Balcony — sunset look: warm golden light floor aur railing par


   Har prompt mein:
   - Pehli line: "Use the attached photo as the exact scene. Only change the lighting and
   time of day — do not change anything else."
   - Us photo ki Room Lock line word-to-word (exterior, living room, balcony)
   - Bahar ka view, buildings, trees, furniture — sab same rahe. Koi naya view, pool, garden
   ya extra cheez add nahi karni
   - Same camera angle, same phone-photo look, no people, no text


   Teeno prompts English mein, copy-paste ready.



  Lesson 05 · Galti wala prompt (Flow Video, sirf comparison ke liye)


   camera flies through the whole house fast



  Lesson 05 · Message 1: Walkthrough + day-to-evening prompts (ChatGPT)


   Ab walkthrough video banana hai. Apni shot list ke hisaab se Google Flow Video (Frames
   mode, start frame = photo) ke liye 4 video prompts likho, 9:16, 8 seconds:


   1. Building exterior (golden evening wali photo) — slow push-in toward the building
   2. Living room (modern Indian staged, din wali) — slow smooth glide from left to right
   3. Master bedroom (staged) — slow pan across the room
   4. Balcony (sunset wali) — slow push-in toward the railing and the view


   Har prompt mein:
   - "Only the camera moves. The room, furniture, walls, windows and view stay exactly the
   same."
   - Ek hi simple, slow camera move — koi fast move ya 360 nahi
   - No people, no new objects, no text, no music
   - Real phone-video jaisa natural look


   Ek 5th prompt bhi do: living room ka day-to-evening transition — start frame din wali
   photo, end frame evening wali photo, camera static, lights dheere-dheere on hoti hain.


   Saare prompts English mein, copy-paste ready.

  Lesson 06 · Message 1: Fast montage prompts (ChatGPT)


   Ab is property ka ek FAST, high-energy cinematic montage banana hai — jaise luxury
   property trailers aur viral Instagram real-estate reels mein hota hai. Aise shots jo phone
   se shoot hi nahi ho sakte.


   Google Flow Video (Frames mode, start frame = meri photo) ke liye 5 prompts likho, 9:16, 4
   seconds each:


   1. Building exterior (golden evening) — fast FPV drone rise from street level up the
   building facade
   2. Living room (modern Indian staged, evening) — rapid low-angle push-in toward the sofa,
   almost touching the floor
   3. Living room detail — extreme close-up macro on the lamp and coffee table, then fast
   pull-back reveal of the room
   4. Master bedroom (staged) — quick whip pan into a fast half-arc around the bed
   5. Balcony (sunset) — fast FPV push through the balcony door straight to the railing,
   ending on the real view


   Har prompt mein:
   - Camera speed FAST aur aggressive, speed-ramp feel (fast start, slow-motion end), motion
   blur on the fast part, crisp sharp frame at the end
   - Har clip mein sirf EK fast move — taaki room morph na ho
   - Us photo ki Room Lock line word-to-word
   - "The room, furniture, walls, windows and outside view stay exactly the same. Do not add
   any new rooms, objects, views or amenities."
   - Look: high-end cinematic real-estate film, punchy contrast, no people, no text, no music


   Har prompt ke start mein camera move ek line mein likho (jaise "FAST FPV DRONE RISE").
   Saare prompts English mein, copy-paste ready.



  Lesson 07 · Message 1: Reel text, disclaimer, caption, music (ChatGPT)


   Ab is property ki final listing reel CapCut mein banani hai (9:16, 20–25 seconds). Iske
   liye mujhe ye do:


   1. On-screen text — 5–6 chhoti lines (har line max 5 words), reel ke order mein: hook,
   location, size/BHK, 2 highlights, aur end card. Sirf wahi details jo maine brief mein di
   thi, kuch apni taraf se mat jodna. Price ki jagah "Price on request" likho.
   2. Ek line ka "Virtually Staged" disclaimer jo staged clips par dikhega.
   3. Instagram caption (Hinglish, 3–4 lines) + 8 relevant hashtags.
   4. Music ka mood — kis type ka track lagaun (energetic / luxury / chill).


   Simple rakho, cheesy mat banana.



  Lesson 08 · Message 1: Service packages


   Ab main ye kaam brokers aur builders ke liye service ke roop mein dena chahta hoon. Mere
   liye ek simple service package banao — 3 options (Basic, Standard, Premium) — har option
   mein kya deliver hoga: kitni staged photos, light upgrade, walkthrough clips, cinematic
   montage, final reel. Price mat likhna, woh main khud decide karunga. Har package mein
   "Virtually Staged" disclosure included ho.

  Lesson 08 · Message 2: Client checklist


   Ab ek short client checklist banao — kaam shuru karne se pehle broker se kya-kya lena hai:
   kaunsi photos, kaunse angles, property ki asli details, aur ek line ki permission ki in
   photos par AI staging hogi aur disclosure lagega. WhatsApp par bhejne layak, simple
   Hinglish mein.



  Lesson 08 · Message 3: Broker pitch


   Ab ek broker ko bhejne ke liye pehla WhatsApp message likho — Hinglish, friendly, 4–5
   lines. Usme bolo ki main khaali flat ki photos se furnished, evening look aur cinematic
   reel bana sakta hoon, aur ek sample reel attach hai. Koi jhootha promise mat karna —
   "zyada sales", "guaranteed buyers" jaisa kuch nahi.



  Lesson 08 · Message 4: Honesty rules


   Last: 5 rules likho jo har AI real estate video mein follow karne chahiye — taaki buyer ko
   kabhi dhokha na ho. Jaise disclosure, room ka structure na badalna, nakli view ya
   amenities na dikhana. Ek-ek line mein.


 5 Honesty Rules
    • Har AI-staged photo/video par visible 'Virtually Staged' label.
    • Room ka asli size, walls, doors, windows, flooring aur layout kabhi mat badlo.
    • Balcony/window ke bahar nakli view (greenery, skyline, garden, sea) mat dikhao.
    • Jo amenity asli mein nahi hai (pool, lift, parking, gym) use feature ki tarah mat dikhao.
    • Location, area, floor, features hamesha verify karo; AI visuals ko asli footage bata kar mat
     do.
