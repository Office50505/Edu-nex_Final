COURSE DESCRIPTION
Learn to create, review and publish a WhatsApp-focused landing page for a local business using ChatGPT, Google Flow, VS Code, Chrome and Netlify. Follow the Sweet Crumbs home-bakery demonstration from planning and copywriting through images, HTML, mobile improvements and publishing. Then build a truthful portfolio and practice finding and communicating with prospective clients. The course emphasizes accurate business details, genuine reviews and realistic expectations; it does not promise sales or income.

LESSON INDEX AND ENGLISH KNOWLEDGE
LESSON 01: Course Introduction
OBJECTIVE: Define a landing page, its single conversion goal and the full no-code course journey.
CONCEPTS: A landing page is a focused page built around one action, here a WhatsApp enquiry or order. Local businesses may use it alongside social media to organize offers, examples and contact information. Sweet Crumbs is a sample home bakery in Indore.
WORKFLOW: Identify three nearby business types that could use a landing page. Explain their likely page goal. List ChatGPT, Google Flow, VS Code, Chrome, Netlify, Maps and Sheets as the course tools.
ASSIGNMENT: Write down three business categories and one realistic goal for each.
CAUTION: Do not guarantee orders or earnings.

LESSON 02: Page Planning
OBJECTIVE: Turn verified business details into an offer, page outline and final headline.
CONCEPTS: Collect business name, products or services, city, target customers and desired action. Ask for one offer, three customer problems, a page-section order and five headline options. The example selected headline is “Birthday Ho Ya Anniversary, Cake Ho Aapki Pasand Ka!”
WORKFLOW: Enter Sweet Crumbs sample details into the Lesson 02 planning prompt. Review five headline options and select one. Save the offer, customer problems and section sequence.
ASSIGNMENT: Prepare a business brief and choose a final headline.
CAUTION: A vague request like “make a landing page” lacks the information needed for accurate copy.

LESSON 03: Landing Page Copy
OBJECTIVE: Write useful text for every section without invented claims.
CONCEPTS: Continue in the same ChatGPT conversation. Draft Hero, Why Us, four cake descriptions, How to Order, reviews placeholder, FAQ and final CTA. Use genuine customer reviews only when available. Owner decides prices.
WORKFLOW: Replace [number] with the chosen headline option. Request section-by-section copy in simple language. Verify each benefit and FAQ against the business. Save the full copy in page order.
ASSIGNMENT: Finish and proofread the complete page copy.
CAUTION: Do not invent reviews, customer numbers or prices.

LESSON 04: Images with Google Flow
OBJECTIVE: Create three illustrative 16:9 images and save exact filenames.
CONCEPTS: The course uses a birthday cake hero image, a four-cake flat-lay and hands decorating a cake in a home kitchen. Prompts request realistic food photography without text, faces or logos.
WORKFLOW: Use ChatGPT to draft the three Google Flow / Nano Banana Pro prompts. Generate the images and review visual accuracy. Save as hero.jpg, cakes.jpg and kitchen.jpg inside sweet-crumbs.
ASSIGNMENT: Prepare the three correctly named images.
CAUTION: AI demonstration images must not be passed off as genuine client-product photos.

LESSON 05: Generate HTML Code
OBJECTIVE: Build a single-file landing page with ChatGPT and verify its WhatsApp CTA.
CONCEPTS: The page uses index.html with embedded CSS, cream background, brown text, pink buttons, the three named images, and a floating WhatsApp button. The WhatsApp link requires an actual approved phone number.
WORKFLOW: Ask ChatGPT for complete index.html code. Put it beside the three images in sweet-crumbs. Open in Chrome. Click every WhatsApp button and confirm the correct number and message.
ASSIGNMENT: Create and locally test the page.
CAUTION: Never publish the example placeholder 91XXXXXXXXXX.

LESSON 06: Mobile Check and Changes
OBJECTIVE: Fix broken images, inspect mobile layout and request controlled revisions.
CONCEPTS: A filename mismatch such as cake.jpg versus cakes.jpg can hide an image. Chrome DevTools supports mobile preview. Sample changes include real contact details, owner-supplied prices, a delivery strip and larger mobile tap targets.
WORKFLOW: Check image paths and refresh. Open Chrome DevTools phone view. Request targeted changes with “keep everything else the same.” Replace the code carefully, save and inspect again.
ASSIGNMENT: Review mobile layout and apply two changes.
CAUTION: Do not introduce unapproved prices or delivery promises.

LESSON 07: Publish on Netlify
OBJECTIVE: Deploy the complete folder and verify the live page.
CONCEPTS: Netlify Drop accepts a folder containing index.html and assets. A published netlify.app link can be renamed when available. Updates require redeploying the revised folder.
WORKFLOW: Upload the entire sweet-crumbs folder, not only index.html. Open the published URL, test images and WhatsApp links, then save the URL. For edits, upload the revised full folder.
ASSIGNMENT: Publish and test a live demonstration page.
CAUTION: Netlify interfaces and plan limits can change; confirm current options.

LESSON 08: Build a Portfolio
OBJECTIVE: Create a truthful portfolio for presenting real and demonstration projects.
CONCEPTS: The sample QuickPage Studio portfolio uses a dark theme, white text and lime accent. Sections include hero, services, Sweet Crumbs sample card, future-client placeholder, process, packages and contact. Its image is sweet-crumbs.png.
WORKFLOW: Generate a one-file portfolio page. Put index.html and sweet-crumbs.png in my-portfolio. Add only genuine work or clearly labeled demos. Publish the folder and verify the sample link.
ASSIGNMENT: Publish a portfolio and save its URL.
CAUTION: Never invent clients, reviews or performance results.

LESSON 09: Find Clients
OBJECTIVE: Research suitable local businesses and send personalized outreach.
CONCEPTS: The course suggests home bakers, salons, tiffin providers, coaching, car wash and event decorators. Google Maps helps identify prospects; a Google Sheet tracks business type, area, website status, map link and outreach status.
WORKFLOW: Find and verify ten businesses. Create a spreadsheet. Draft a short personalized first WhatsApp message with a real portfolio link and a single polite follow-up after two days.
ASSIGNMENT: Build a verified prospect list and two messages.
CAUTION: Do not bulk-spam or invent praise and business facts.

LESSON 10: Handle Client Objections
OBJECTIVE: Explain the value of a WhatsApp landing page and negotiate professionally.
CONCEPTS: The sample client asks why a page is needed alongside Instagram. Example packages are Starter ₹2,999 (one page) and Business ₹5,999 (one page, two revisions, WhatsApp setup). A sample payment structure is 50% advance and 50% after going live.
WORKFLOW: Practice a friendly response explaining the complementary roles of Instagram and the page. Answer a ₹1,500 counteroffer without automatically lowering price. Track agreed scope and payments.
ASSIGNMENT: Draft the two client replies and payment terms.
CAUTION: Course example prices are not universal; never promise results.

LESSON 11: Course Recap
OBJECTIVE: Complete the workflow and prepare a client-ready demonstration.
CONCEPTS: The sequence is Plan → Copy → Images → Code → Mobile Check → Publish → Portfolio → Clients → Deal. Accurate filenames, links, business details and honest claims matter throughout.
WORKFLOW: Audit the page brief, copy, images, CTA, mobile layout and live link. Verify the portfolio accurately describes the project. Prepare a short client handoff and outreach message.
ASSIGNMENT: Finish a published page, portfolio link and handoff checklist.
CAUTION: No guaranteed clients, orders or income.
