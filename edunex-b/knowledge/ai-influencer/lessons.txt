LESSON 4 — Prompt Likhna Seekho DONE

Aaj hum prompt likhna seekhenge. Video ke end tak aapke paas ek ready formula hoga — jisse Flow se exactly
wahi photo banegi jo aap chahte ho. Ye lesson chhota hai, lekin pure course ka sabse important skill yahi hai.
Kyun? Kyunki AI ka pura game prompt pe chalta hai. Achha prompt — achhi photo. Kharab prompt — kharab
photo. Simple. Aur aage jab aap clients ke liye product shoots karoge — wahan bhi yahi formula lagega. Jo
banda achha prompt likhna jaanta hai, wo hi 5 se 20 hazaar wale projects deliver kar paata hai. Baaki log try
karte reh jaate hain. Toh aaj ka formula dhyan se samjho. Prompt ke 5 hisse hote hain. Bas 5.
Pehla — kaun. Matlab character kaun hai. age, gender, nationality.
Doosra — face details. Aankhein, baal, skin, smile.
Teesra — kapde. Kya pehna hai.
Chautha — jagah. Kahan khadi hai. Background kya hai.
Paanchwa — style. Photo kaisi lagni chahiye — realistic, professional, casual. Ab dono ka farak dekho.
Chalo screen pe aa jao mere saath.
(screen recording start — Flow khula hai)
Pehle kharab prompt likhta hu: 'ek sundar ladki ki photo banao' Generate karo... Dekho result. Har baar alag
ladki aayegi. Kuch bhi control nahi. Ab formula wala prompt: 'A 22 year old Indian woman, oval face, brown
eyes, long black hair, wearing a yellow kurti, standing in a cafe, natural lighting, realistic photo' (prompt
screen pe bada dikhao) Generate karo... Ab dekho farak. Wahi mila jo maanga tha. Paanchon hisse gino is
prompt mein — kaun, face, kapde, jagah, style. Sab hai. Ek aur kaam ki baat. Prompt English mein hi likhna. AI
English samajhta hai sabse achha. English weak hai? Koi baat nahi — Flow se hi bolo: 'is Hindi line ko English
prompt banao.' Wo khud bana dega. AI se hi AI ka kaam lo. Ab ek common galti. Log prompt mein 'beautiful',
'gorgeous', 'stunning' jaise words bhar dete hain. Inse kuch nahi hota. AI ko 'beautiful' ka matlab nahi pata —
usko details chahiye. 'Beautiful' hatao, 'oval face, brown eyes' likho. Detail hi beauty hai.
Aaj ka kaam simple hai — apne character ke liye 5 prompts likho, paanchon hisse daal ke. Har prompt mein
sirf jagah aur kapde badlo. Phir teeno generate karke dekho. 15 minute ka kaam hai. Ye 5 prompts sambhal ke
rakhna — agle lesson mein inhi se hum same face wali alag alag photos banayenge. Wahi asli consistency ka
kaam shuru hoga.
Milte hain agle lesson mein.

LESSON 6 — Outfits Badalna DONE

Aaj hum apne character ka pura wardrobe banayenge. Video ke end tak aapke paas 4 alag style ki photos
hongi — casual, ethnic, western, aur party wear. Ab aap sochoge — kapde badalna toh pichle lesson mein
seekh liya. Aaj naya kya hai? Naya ye hai — aaj hum soch samajh ke wardrobe banayenge, random nahi.
Kyunki Instagram pe har outfit ka apna kaam hota hai. Aur fashion brands ke liye toh ye direct kamai hai. Kurti
bechne wala brand chahta hai ki model kurti mein dikhe. Jeans wala brand jeans mein. Jo creator har style
carry kar sakta hai — usko har type ka brand kaam deta hai. Matlab zyada styles, zyada clients.
Toh chalo 4 style ka system samjho.
Pehla — Casual. Jeans, t-shirt, simple look. Ye daily content ke liye hai. Sabse zyada yahi use hoga.
Doosra — Ethnic. Kurti, saree, salwar suit. Indian audience isse sabse zyada connect karti hai. Festival season
mein toh yahi chalega.
Teesra — Western. Dress, formal shirt, office look. Ye premium feel deta hai.
Chautha — Party wear. Shaadi, function, evening look. Occasion content ke liye.
Chalo screen pe aa jao mere saath.
(screen recording start — Flow khula hai) Reference photo upload karo — wahi original wali. Aur prompt ka
pattern same rahega, bas outfit wala hissa badlega. Casual ke liye: 'Use this exact face. Same woman, same
facial features. Now she is wearing blue jeans and a white t-shirt, casual look, standing near a window, natural
light, realistic photo' Generate. Ethnic ke liye bas outfit badlo: '...wearing a red and gold kurti with jhumka
earrings, festive look...' Generate. (dono results dikhao) Ek cheez notice karo — maine sirf kapde nahi likhe,
accessories bhi likhi. Jhumka, earrings, watch — ye chhoti details photo ko real banati hain. Western:
'...wearing a black formal dress, office look, standing in a modern office...' Aur party wear: '...wearing a heavy
embroidered lehenga, wedding guest look, evening lighting...' (chaaro photos ek saath dikhao) Dekho — chaar
style, chaar mood. Aur chaaro mein face same. Ab aapka character kisi bhi type ka content bana sakta hai. Ek

Rule — outfit ki 2-3 cheezein bas. Main kapda, ek accessory, aur lighting. Khatam.
Aaj ka kaam simple hai — apne character ki 4 photos banao, chaaro style mein. Casual, ethnic, western, party.
20 minute ka kaam hai. Ab aapke paas total 9-10 photos ho gayi hongi. Content bank ban raha hai.

Aaj hum apne character ko duniya ghumayenge. Ghar baithe. Video ke end tak wo Goa ke beach pe bhi hogi,

kharcha — zero. Aur yahi cheez brands ko bhi chahiye. Travel brands, cafe, hotels, resorts — sab chahte hain
unki jagah pe model dikhe. Jab aap bologe 'main aapki location pe shoot kara sakta hu bina aaye' — unke liye
ye sasta aur fast hai. Aapke liye ye paisa hai.
Toh chalo, locations ka system samjho. Instagram ke liye 5 type ki locations sabse zyada kaam aati hain. Pehli
— Ghar. Bedroom, balcony, kitchen. Ye relatable content hai — log connect karte hain. Doosri — Cafe. Coffee
shop ka aesthetic look. Reels ke liye perfect. Teesri — Street. City ki road, market, colorful walls. Fashion
content yahan chalta hai. Chauthi — Nature. Beach, pahad, garden. Travel content ka base. Paanchwi —
Premium. Hotel lobby, rooftop, pool side. Ye luxury feel deta hai — brands ko yahi chahiye.
Chalo screen pe aa jao mere saath.
(screen recording start — Flow khula hai) Reference photo upload karo. Pattern wahi — bas location wala hissa
badlega. Beach ke liye: 'Use this exact face. Same woman, same facial features. Now she is standing on a
beach in Goa, wearing a flowy summer dress, sunset in the background, wind in hair, realistic photo' Generate.
(result pe 1-2 second pause) Dekho — sunset, beach, hawa mein baal. Aisa lag raha hai sach mein Goa gayi
hai. Ek cheez notice karo prompt mein — 'sunset in the background, wind in hair.' Maine sirf jagah nahi likhi,
mahaul likha. Yahi photo ko zinda banata hai. Ab street: '...walking on a busy Mumbai street, colorful shops in
background, candid walking pose...' Aur cafe: '...sitting in an aesthetic cafe, holding a coffee cup, plants and
warm lights in background...' (teeno results dikhao) Teen shehar, teen mood — aur kharcha zero. Ek pro tip.
Location ke saath pose bhi badlo. Beach pe 'walking' ya 'looking at the sea'. Cafe mein 'sitting, holding cup'.
Street pe 'candid walking'. Har jagah khadi hokar camera dekhna — nakli lagta hai. Real influencers candid
photos daalte hain.

Aaj ka kaam simple hai — apne character ki 5 photos banao, paanchon location type mein. Ghar, cafe, street,
nature, premium. 25 minute ka kaam hai. Ab aapke paas 15 ke aas paas photos ho gayi hongi — alag outfits,

LESSON 8 — Realistic Skin aur DONE

Aaj hum sabse badi problem fix karenge — plastic wali AI look. Video ke end tak aapki photos aisi lagengi jaise
kisi ne camera se kheenchi hon.
Pehle problem samjho. AI photos aksar zyada perfect hoti hain. Skin bilkul chikni, ek bhi daag nahi, baal ek ek
jagah set. Aur yahi problem hai — insaan aise nahi dikhte. Log photo dekh ke 2 second mein pakad lete hain
— 'ye AI hai.' Aur jaise hi pakda gaya, trust khatam. Followers nahi badhenge.
Aur brands ke liye toh ye seedha rejection hai — koi brand nakli dikhne wali photo pe paise nahi lagata. Real
dikhna hi is business ki asli currency hai. Toh aaj ka formula — perfection hatao, reality daalo. Iske liye prompt
mein 4 cheezein add karni hain. Pehli — skin texture. 'Natural skin texture, visible pores' — matlab skin pe
halki asli bandagi dikhe. Doosri — imperfections. 'Slight imperfections, few flyaway hairs' — thode bikhre baal,
halka sa asli-pan. Teesri — camera details. 'Shot on iPhone' ya 'shot on DSLR, 50mm lens' — AI ko camera ka
naam do, wo photo waisa hi banata hai. Chauthi — natural lighting. 'Soft natural light' — studio wali flat
lighting nakli lagti hai.
Chalo screen pe aa jao mere saath.
(screen recording start — Flow khula hai)
Pehle bina in details ke ek photo banati hu — jaise ab tak banate the. (generate karo, result dikhao) Achhi hai,
lekin dekho — skin plastic jaisi chamak rahi hai. Ye AI look hai. Ab wahi prompt, 4 nayi cheezon ke saath: 'Use
this exact face. Same woman, same facial features. Sitting in a cafe, wearing a white shirt. Natural skin
texture, visible pores, slight imperfections, few flyaway hairs, shot on iPhone, soft natural light, realistic candid
photo' (prompt screen pe bada dikhao) Generate karo. (dono photos side by side dikhao) Farak dekho. Pehli
wali — magazine ka poster. Doosri wali — kisi dost ne photo kheenchi ho jaisi. Aur Instagram pe doosri wali hi
chalti hai. Ek aur kaam ka word — 'candid'. Iska matlab bina pose kiye, natural moment. 'Candid photo' likhne
se pose bhi natural aata hai aur photo aur real lagti hai.
Ab ek honest baat. Kabhi kabhi in sab ke baad bhi haath ya ungliyan ajeeb aayengi — 6 ungliyan, mude hue
haath. Ye AI ki sabse purani kamzori hai. Aisa aaye toh photo dobara generate karo, ya aisi pose maango jisme
haath kam dikhe — 'hands in pockets' ya 'holding a bag'.
Ab common galti. Log realistic banane ke chakkar mein prompt mein 'ultra realistic, hyper realistic, 8K, ultra
HD' bhar dete hain. Ulta asar hota hai — photo aur zyada AI jaisi lagti hai. Ye words hatao. 'Realistic candid
photo, shot on iPhone' — bas itna kaafi hai.
Aaj ka kaam simple hai — apni purani 3 photos utho, aur wahi prompts in 4 nayi details ke saath dobara
generate karo. Pehli aur nayi photo compare karo. Farak khud dikh jayega. 15-20 minute ka kaam. Aaj ke baad
aapki har photo mein ye 4 cheezein hamesha rahengi. Ye aapka permanent formula hai.

— pura repair system.

LESSON 9 — Photo Quality Fix DONE

Aaj hum photo repair karna seekhenge. Kharab haath, ajeeb aankhein, bigda background — sab theek karna.
Video ke end tak aapke paas ek complete checklist hogi — kaunsi photo rakhni hai, kaunsi phenkni hai, aur
kaunsi repair karni hai. Ye lesson kyun zaroori hai? Kyunki ek kharab photo aapka pura account duba sakti hai.

checklist samjho. Har photo save karne se pehle 4 cheezein dekho. Pehli — haath aur ungliyan. Gino. Paanch
hain? Shape sahi hai? Doosri — aankhein. Dono ek jagah dekh rahi hain? Ek jaisi hain? Teesri — background.
Peeche ki cheezein sahi hain? Bigde hue words ya toote objects toh nahi? Chauthi — face match. Reference se

Chalo screen pe aa jao mere saath.
(screen recording start — Flow khula hai) Ye dekho — ek photo jisme sab achha hai, lekin haath ajeeb aaya

pyaar mat karo — quality se karo.
Ab common galti. Log repair pe repair karte jaate hain — pehle haath fix, phir aankh fix, phir background fix.

Rule — ek photo pe maximum ek fix. Do problems hain? Delete, nayi banao.
Aaj ka kaam simple hai — apni ab tak ki saari photos kholo aur chaaro checklist points pe check karo. Jo fail ho
— ek fix try karo ya delete karke nayi banao. 20 minute ka kaam hai. Aaj ke baad aapki gallery mein sirf
perfect photos rahengi. Yahi professional standard hai.

LESSON 13 — AI Voice Banana DONE

Teesra — tone. Energetic hai, calm hai, ya friendly. In teeno ko milakar character ki voice personality banti hai.

awaaz ka feel yaad rakhte hain.

Rule — simple aur napi-tuli details: pitch (soft/deep), accent (Indian/neutral), pace (slow/medium/fast). Bas.
Jitna simple likhoge, utna same milega.
Aaj ka kaam simple hai — Neha ki voice description final karo — pitch, accent, pace. Notes mein save karo

LESSON 16 — Profile Setup (professional) SCRIPT · ~3min

Aaj se Module 4 shuru. Ab tak jo banaya wo factory thi — aaj se dukaan kholenge. Aaj Neha ki professional
profile banegi. Video ke end tak account live hoga, ready to post.
Pehle samjho profile itni badi baat kyun hai. Jab koi aapki reel dekhta hai, wo turant profile pe aata hai — aur 3
second mein decide karta hai follow kare ya nahi. Aur brands to deal se pehle profile 10 baar dekhte hain.
Kharab bio, adhura profile — deal wahin khatam. Profile aapki dukaan ka board hai.
4 cheezein set karni hain. Pehla — username. Chhota, yaad rehne wala, character ke naam se juda — jaise
neha.mumbai. Numbers aur underscores mat daalo, spam jaisa lagta hai. Naam liya hua ho to ek word jodo —
style, diaries.
Doosra — profile photo. Neha ki sabse achhi close-up photo, jisme face saaf dikhe. Full body mat lena —
profile chhoti dikhti hai, door se sirf face pehchana jaata hai.
Teesra — bio, 3 lines. Pehli: wo kaun hai — Fashion and Travel Creator. Doosri: kahan se — Mumbai. Teesri:
personality ya hook. Ek-do emoji bas. Bio mein AI likhna optional hai — kuch log likhte hain, curiosity mein log
follow karte hain. Par kisi ke puchne pe jhooth mat bolna — curiosity theek hai, dhokha nahi.
Chautha — account type. Settings mein Professional Account, phir Creator. Free hai, aur analytics deta hai —
kaunsi reel kitne logon ne dekhi, followers kab active hain. Bina data ke growth andhere mein teer hai.
Aur ek chhoti baat — jab post karna shuru karo, pehli posts random mat daalna. Thoda soch ke daalna taaki
profile khulte hi saaf aur professional lage, bikhra hua nahi.
Ab common galti. Log account banate hi 10 photos ek saath thok dete hain, excitement mein. Naye account
se app spam ka shak karta hai, aur reach pehle din se dab jaati hai. Rule — aaj sirf setup aur zyada se zyada
ek pehli post. Baaki posting schedule se jayega, agla lesson.
Aaj ka kaam — profile taiyaar karo: username, close-up profile photo, 3-line bio, Creator account on. Phir ek
achhi pehli post daal do. 20 minute ka kaam.
Agle lesson mein posting schedule aur captions-hashtags ka system banayenge — taaki roz kya aur kab post
karna hai, sochna na pade. Milte hain agle lesson mein.

LESSON 17 — Posting Schedule + Captions & Hashtags SCRIPT · ~3min

Aaj hum posting ka pura system banayenge — kab post karna, kitna, kaunse time pe, aur har post ke saath
jaane wale do zaroori cheezein: caption aur hashtags. Video ke end tak aapke paas ek fixed weekly schedule
hoga jise bas follow karna hai.
Pehle samjho schedule itna zaroori kyun hai. App ka algorithm sabse zyada ek cheez dekhta hai — regularity.
Roz aane wala account uski nazar mein serious hai. Kabhi 5 posts phir 10 din gayab — aisa account timepass
hai, aur timepass ko reach nahi milti.
Toh schedule simple rakho. Hafte mein 3 reels — Lesson 15 wale formats se, teen alag. Rotation account ko
fresh rakhta hai. Timing — jab aapke followers active hon, wahi Creator analytics batata hai. Aam taur pe
subah 8-10 ya shaam 7-10 achha chalta hai. Ek fixed din-time chuno, jaise Mon-Wed-Fri shaam 8 baje, aur usi
pe tik jao.
Ab caption aur hashtags — chhoti cheezein, par reach mein bada farak. Dono ka kaam alag hai. Hashtag app
ko batata hai ye post kis baare mein hai, kisko dikhani chahiye. Caption dekhne wale ko rokta hai — ruko,
padho, comment karo. Ek machine ke liye, ek insaan ke liye. Dono chahiye.
Caption formula. Pehli line hook honi chahiye — sabse zaroori, kyunki wahi 'more' se pehle dikhti hai. Ek sawal
ya bold baat. Phir 1-2 line value ya story. Aur end pe ek call to action — comment karo, save karo, follow karo.
Long paragraph mat likho, log padhte nahi.
Hashtags. Galat soch — 30 bade hashtags daal do. Ismein aapki chhoti post kho jaati hai. Sahi tareeka — mix.
Kuch bade (lakhon posts wale), kuch medium, aur kuch chhote niche wale jahan aap actually dikh sakte ho.
10-15 relevant hashtags kaafi hain, 30 bekaar bhare hue nahi. Har baar ek jaisa set copy-paste mat karo —
app ko spam lagta hai, 2-3 sets banao aur rotate karo.
Ab common galti. Log caption ko baad ki soch samajhte hain — video pe mehnat, caption mein 'nice day' likh
dete hain. Adha kaam wahin barbaad. Caption post ka salesman hai.
Aaj ka kaam — apna weekly schedule likho: kaunse din, kaunsa time, kaunse formats. Phir 3 caption templates
aur 3 hashtag sets bana ke notes mein save karo. Ek baar bana lo, phir har post pe bas bharna hai.
Agle lesson mein algorithm ke andar ghusenge — samjhenge kaunsi reel rukti hai aur kaunsi udti hai. Milte
hain agle lesson mein.

LESSON 18 — Reels Algorithm Samjho SCRIPT · ~3min

Aaj hum app ke dimag ke andar ghusenge — algorithm. Wahi cheez jo decide karti hai kaunsi reel 200 views
pe rukegi aur kaunsi 2 lakh tak jayegi. Video ke end tak aap samajh jaoge ye machine kaam kaise karti hai,
aur ise khush kaise rakhna hai.
Pehle ek galat soch todo. Log samajhte hain algorithm dushman hai jo reach dabata hai. Galat. Algorithm ek
dukaandaar hai — uska ek hi kaam hai, log app pe zyada der rukein. Jo reel logon ko rokti hai, algorithm use
aur logon tak le jaata hai. Aap aur algorithm dushman nahi, partner ho — dono ko ek hi cheez chahiye, log
rukein.
Ab machine kaise sochti hai, step by step. Aap reel daalte ho. App use pehle chhote group ko dikhata hai —
jaise 200 logon ko. Ab wo dekhta hai in 200 ne kya kiya. Agar zyada logon ne poori dekhi, dubara dekhi, likeshare-save kiya — app samajhta hai ye achhi hai, aur agle 2000 ko dikhata hai. Agar logon ne pehle second
mein scroll kar diya — app samajhta hai bekaar hai, aur rok deta hai. Har level pe yahi test.
Toh algorithm 3 cheezein sabse zyada dekhta hai. Ek — watch time, kitni der dekhi. Isi liye pehla second sabse
zaroori hai, aur video chhoti-tight honi chahiye. Do — completion aur re-watch, poori dekhi ya loop hui. Isi liye
reel ka end shuru se jude to loop banta hai. Teen — shares aur saves, kyunki jo cheez log doston ko bhejte
hain ya save karte hain wahi algorithm ke liye sona hai. Likes se zyada saves aur shares matter karte hain.
Ek aur cheez — pehle 1 ghante ka engagement. Post karte hi agar log jaldi react karte hain, algorithm ko
signal milta hai ye hot hai. Isi liye active time pe post karna zaroori hai, aur pehle ghante mein comments ka
reply dena.
Ab common galti. Log har baar naya kuch try karte hain — ek reel chali to bhi format badal dete hain.
Algorithm confuse ho jaata hai ki account kis baare mein hai. Rule — jo chala, uska pattern samjho aur repeat
karo. Consistency algorithm ko clear signal deti hai.
Aaj ka kaam — apni pichhli reels analytics mein kholo. Dekho sabse zyada kaunsi dekhi gayi, aur uske pehle 2
second mein kya tha. Wahi cheez agli reel mein repeat karo. 15 minute — aaj se data aapka teacher hai.

LESSON 19 — 0 se 1000 Followers + Engagement SCRIPT · ~3min

Aaj main aapko 0 se 1000 followers ka pura roadmap dungi, aur unhe rokne-jodne ka engagement system bhi.
Video ke end tak aapko exactly pata hoga agle 2 mahine kya karna hai.
Pehle samjho 1000 ka number khaas kyun hai. Do wajah. Ek — 1000 pe app aapko serious lena shuru karta
hai, reach khulti hai. Do, aur ye badi hai — 1000 se brands ki duniya khulti hai. Aapko lagega 1000 kuch nahi,
brands to lakhon wale dhundhte hain. Galat. Nano-influencers — 1000 se 10000 wale — brands ke sabse
pasandeeda hain, kyunki engagement sabse zyada aur rate sasta.
Roadmap. Bhool jao ki 1000 ek pahaad hai — ise hafton mein todo. Har hafte 3 reels, teen alag formats. Do
mahine mein 24 reels. Ismein se 2-3 chalengi, aur wahi 1000 tak le aayengi. Bas 3 cheezein consistent rakho
— regularity, formats rotation, aur pehle ghante ka engagement. Shuru ke followers aapke apne circle se
aayenge — koi sharam nahi, wahi base hai.
Ab asli khel — followers laane ke baad unhe rokna aur jodna. Isi ko engagement kehte hain, aur ye followers
se zyada keemti hai brands ke liye. Engagement matlab log sirf aate nahi, kuch karte hain — like, comment,
reply, share. App ki nazar mein engaged account hi zinda account hai.
Roz ka 20-minute routine. Ek — apni har post ke comments ka reply do, khaas kar pehle ghante mein. Reply se
comment badhta hai, comment se reach. Do — apni story mein roz kuch daalo — poll, sawal, behind the
scenes. Stories followers ko roz aapse jodti hain aur bina reach khoye active rakhti hain. Teen — apne niche ke
doosre creators aur followers ke posts pe genuine comment karo, roz 10-15. Isse naye log aap tak aate hain.
Ye peecha karo, spam nahi — asli baat.
Ab common galti. Log post karke gayab ho jaate hain — daala aur bhool gaye. Instagram do-tarfa hai. Jo sirf
post karta hai, community nahi banti; jo reply karta, comment karta, judta hai — wahi badhta hai.
Aaj ka kaam — do cheezein. Ek, apna 8-hafte ka reel plan kaagaz pe likho: har hafte 3 reels, teen formats. Do,
aaj ka 20-minute engagement routine abhi karo — apne comments ke reply, ek story, aur 10 doosron ke posts
pe comment.

LESSON 20 — Product Photo Basics SCRIPT · ~3min

Aaj se Module 5 shuru, aur aaj se course ka rang badal jaata hai — kyunki aaj se wo seekhenge jiske log
seedha paise dete hain. Product photography. Video ke end tak aapke paas pehli product photo hogi — Neha
ke haath mein ek asli product.
Pehle market samjho. Har online seller ko product photos chahiye — Amazon wala, Flipkart wala, Instagram pe
kapde bechne wali didi, Shopify store wala. Sabko. Aur traditional shoot ka kharcha suno — model ka charge,
photographer, studio, ek din. Hazaaron rupaye ek shoot ke. Aap wahi kaam Flow se ghar baithe, ek din mein
kai baar kar sakte ho — isi liye ye seedhi kamai hai.
Ab basic samjho. Product photo ka ek hi kaam hai — product ko saaf, achha, bharose layak dikhana. Do type
hote hain: clean shot, jahan product plain background pe alag dikhe; aur lifestyle shot, jahan product zindagi
mein istemaal hota dikhe. Aaj clean shot, jo base hai.
Kaise banate hain. Aapke paas do cheezein hain — Neha, aur product ki photo jo client bhejta hai. Flow mein
dono ko jodna hai: Neha product ko haath mein pakde, ya use karti dikhe. Prompt mein teen cheezein clear
rakho — product bilkul waisa jaisa client ki photo mein hai, Neha ka wahi consistent face, aur ek saaf simple
background jahan product ubhre. Magic line yaad rakho — same face — taaki har product photo mein Neha ek
jaisi rahe.
Ek zaroori baat — product ki shape, colour, aur label bilkul same rehna chahiye. Client ka product agar red
bottle hai to red hi rahe, aur uspe likha naam badle nahi. Ismein AI galti karta hai — isi liye result ko dhyaan se
check karo, aur galat aaye to prompt mein product ki detail aur pakki karo.
Ab common galti. Log product ko chhota, kone mein, ya andhere mein dikha dete hain — nazar Neha pe chali
jaati hai, product pe nahi. Yaad rakho — yahan hero product hai, Neha nahi. Product frame mein bada,
saamne, achhi roshni mein hona chahiye.
Aaj ka kaam — koi bhi ek product lo, kisi cheez ki saaf photo — chai ka cup, bottle, ya cream ka jar. Flow mein
Neha ke saath uski ek clean product photo banao. Product hero rakho, face same rakho, background simple.
20 minute ka kaam.
Agle lesson mein isko professional studio level pe le jayenge — lighting, background, angles ka system. Milte

LESSON 21 — Professional Studio Shots SCRIPT · ~3min

Aaj hum product photos ko professional studio level pe le jayenge. Lighting, background, aur angles — teeno
ka system. Video ke end tak aap wahi photos bana paoge jo bade brands apne catalogs mein use karte hain.
Pehle samjho studio shot ka matlab kya hai. Kal wali clean shot achhi thi, par studio shot usse ek level upar
hai. Farak details mein hai: sahi lighting jo product ko chamkaye, sahi background jo product ko ubhaare, aur
sahi angle jo product ki best side dikhaye. Yahi teen cheezein saste aur mehnge look ka farak banati hain.
Pehla — lighting. Product photo mein lighting sabse badi cheez hai. Prompt mein soft studio lighting maango
— aisi roshni jo product pe hard shadow na daale, aur uski shape saaf dikhaye. Bottle, jar, ya gadget ho to
halki shine achhi lagti hai. Andhera ya flat light product ko sasta dikhata hai.
Doosra — background. Studio shot ke liye plain, clean background — white, grey, ya product ke rang se match
karta ek soft shade. Background kabhi product se zyada dhyaan na kheenche. Ek trick — thoda soft shadow
product ke neeche rakho, isse product 'rakha hua' dikhta hai, hawa mein tairta nahi.
Teesra — angle. Har product ka ek best angle hota hai. Bottle aur jar aankh ke level pe seedha; jewellery aur
watch thoda upar se close; shoes ka side. Ek product ke 2-3 angle banao — front, thoda tilt, aur ek close-up
detail. Client ko choice dena professional lagta hai.
Ab prompt mein sab jodo — product exactly same, Neha ka same face agar wo pakde ho, soft studio lighting,
clean background, aur angle bataao. Aur wahi rule — product hero, saaf, sharp.
Ab common galti. Log ek hi photo banake bhej dete hain. Client ko lagta hai option nhi hai. Rule — har product
ke kam se kam 3 variations do: alag angle, ya sirf product, ya Neha ke saath. Zyada choice, zyada
professional.
Aaj ka kaam — kal wale product ko studio level pe le jao. Soft lighting, clean background, aur 3 alag angles
banao. Ek set taiyaar karo jaise ek asli catalog ke liye. 20-25 minute.
Agle lesson mein lifestyle shots seekhenge — product ko asli zindagi mein dikhana, aur platform-ready
banana. Milte hain agle lesson mein.

LESSON 22 — Lifestyle + Platform-Ready Shots SCRIPT · ~3min

Aaj hum do cheezein seekhenge — lifestyle shots, aur photos ko platform-ready banana. Lifestyle product
photography ka sabse mehnga hissa hai, aur platform rules wo cheez hai jo client ko turant impress karti hai.
Video ke end tak dono aa jayenge.
Pehle lifestyle samjho. Studio shot product ko dikhata hai — lifestyle shot product ke saath zindagi dikhata hai.
Watch white background pe studio hai. Wahi watch Neha ki kalai pe, cafe mein coffee ke saath — lifestyle. Aur
log cheez nahi, cheez ke saath ki feeling khareedte hain. Isi liye brands lifestyle ke zyada paise dete hain.
Kaise banate hain. Neha ko product ke saath asli scene mein rakho — skincare bathroom mirror ke saamne,
sunglasses cafe ki dhoop mein, kurti street pe. Prompt mein teen cheezein: product bilkul same, Neha ka same
face, aur ek asli natural setting jahan product istemaal ho raha lage. Candid feel maango — posed studio nahi.
Product phir bhi saaf dikhe, chhupe nahi.
Ab platform-ready. Client ko sirf achhi photo nahi, uske platform ke hisaab se sahi photo chahiye. Har platform
ki apni bhasha hai. Amazon aur Flipkart ke main image ka rule — plain pure white background, product frame
mein bada, koi text ya extra cheez nahi. Isi liye pehli clean studio wali white background photo yahan kaam
aati hai. Instagram aur website ke liye lifestyle aur square ya vertical crop chalta hai. Ek smart creator dono
deta hai — Amazon ke liye white, Instagram ke liye lifestyle.
Ek chhoti baat jo bada farak karti hai — size aur crop. Amazon square chahta hai, Instagram feed square ya 4
by 5, reel 9 by 16. Client ko ready size mein dena — usko crop na karna pade — isi se aap yaad reh jaate ho.
Ab common galti. Log ek hi photo har jagah bhej dete hain. Amazon lifestyle reject kar deta hai kyunki
background white nahi; Instagram pe plain white boring lagta hai. Rule — pehle poochho photo kahan chahiye,
phir us platform ke hisaab se banao.
Aaj ka kaam — apne product ki ek lifestyle photo banao Neha ke saath asli scene mein. Phir usi product ki ek
Amazon-ready plain white photo bhi. Do platform, do photo — ek set. 25 minute.

LESSON 23 — Fashion Try-On SCRIPT · ~3min

Aaj hum fashion try-on seekhenge — kisi bhi kapde, jewellery, ya accessory ko Neha ko pehnana. Video ke end
tak aap client ka bheja hua kurti ka flat photo utha ke, wahi kurti Neha ko pehna paoge.
Pehle samjho ye skill itni keemti kyun hai. Online shopping ki sabse badi problem — customer ko pata nahi
kapda pehna hua kaisa lagega. Isi liye fashion mein returns sabse zyada. Aur iska ilaaj — model photos. Jo
kurti model pe dikhti hai, wo flat photo se 3 guna zyada bikti hai. Har chhoti clothing brand, har boutique wali
didi ko ye chahiye, par model ka kharcha uthana mushkil. Aap wahi sasta aur turant de dete ho.
Kaise karte hain. Aapke paas do cheezein — Neha, aur client ka product photo, jaise ek kurti flat rakhi hui.
Flow mein try-on ka matlab — wahi kurti Neha ko pehna do. Prompt mein teen cheezein: kapda bilkul same
rahe — same colour, same design, same print; Neha ka same face; aur ek natural pose ya simple background.
Sabse zaroori — kapde ka design, print aur colour badalna nahi chahiye. Yahin AI galti karta hai — print thoda
change kar deta hai. Result dhyaan se check karo, print match karo.
Kya-kya pehna sakte ho — kurti, saree, dress, top; jewellery jaise earrings aur necklace; aur accessories jaise
sunglasses, bag, watch. Jewellery aur accessories ke liye close-up angle achha kaam karta hai taaki detail
dikhe.
Ek professional touch — ek hi kapde ke 2-3 look do. Ek full body, ek half, ek close-up fabric detail. Client ko
poora idea milta hai, aur wo photos seedha uski website pe ja sakti hain.
Ab common galti. Log kapde ka print ya colour badalne dete hain aur bhej dete hain. Client ke liye ye sabse
bada dhoka hai — jo dikha wo customer ko milega nahi, returns aur badhenge. Rule — product bilkul same,
chahe pose kuch bhi ho.
Aaj ka kaam — kisi kapde ya accessory ki ek flat photo lo — apni ya online se — aur Flow mein Neha ko wahi
pehna ke ek try-on photo banao. Print same rakhna. Phir 2 alag look banao. 20-25 minute.

LESSON 24 — Pehla Client Kaise Laye SCRIPT · ~3min

Aaj ka lesson course ka sabse bada mod hai. Aaj tak aap seekh rahe the — aaj se kamaoge. Pehla client kaise
dhundhna, kaise baat karna, aur kaise haan karwana. Video ke end tak aapke paas pura outreach system
hoga.
Pehle ek dar todo. Log sochte hain client lana matlab salesman banna, ghar ghar dhakke khana. Galat. Aapke
paas wo cheez hai jo hazaaron sellers ko chahiye aur sasti mein mil nahi rahi — professional photos. Aap
maang nahi rahe, ek problem ka hal de rahe ho.
Ab client kahan hai. Sabse aasan jagah — Instagram khud. Chhote sellers dhundho: kapde bechne wali
boutiques, homemade skincare, jewellery, candles, chhote food brands. Inki profiles dekho — jinki photos
kamzor hain, wahi aapke best client hain, kyunki unhe farak turant dikhega. Doosri jagah — local businesses,
Facebook marketplace sellers, aur apne aas-paas ke dukaan wale jinki online presence kamzor hai.
Ab baat kaise karni hai. Cold message ka formula — chhota aur unke baare mein. Teen line. Pehli — ek sachhi
tareef ya unke product ka zikr. Doosri — ek short line ki aap professional product photos banate ho jo sales
badhati hain. Teesri — ek chhoti offer ya sawal, jaise 'ek free sample bana ke dikhau?'. Lambe paragraph aur
'main ye main wo' mat likho — koi nahi padhta.
Sabse taakatwar cheez — free sample. Unhi ke product ki ek photo bina maange bana ke bhej do. Jab wo apni
cheez professional dikhti dekhte hain, aadhi haan wahin ho jaati hai. Ek din mein 5-10 sellers ko aise approach
karo. Number ka khel hai — 20 messages mein 1-2 haan karte hain, aur wahi shuru ke liye kaafi.
Ab common galti. Log ek-do message bhej ke, jawab na aane pe haar jaate hain. Ya har kisi ko ek jaisa copypaste bhejte hain. Rule — roz thoda outreach, har message thoda personal, aur follow-up karo. Ek 'na' ka
matlab agla client, band darwaaza nahi.
Aaj ka kaam — 10 chhote sellers dhundho jinki photos kamzor hain. Un mein se 2 ke liye ek-ek free sample
photo banao, aur teeno line wala message ke saath bhejo. Aaj se roz 5 outreach. 30 minute.

LESSON 25 — Product Shoot ki Pricing SCRIPT · ~3min

Aaj Module 5 ka last lesson — aur sabse zaroori sawal ka jawab: kitne paise maangne hain. Video ke end tak
aapke paas ready rate card hoga. Client puchhega 'kitne loge?' aur aap bina atke jawab doge.
Pehle samjho pricing itni badi baat kyun hai. Yahin do galtiyan hoti hain, dono khatarnak. Pehli — zyada maang
liya, client bhaag gaya. Doosri, aur ye zyada hoti hai — kam maang liya. Kam maangne se sirf kamai nahi girti,
izzat girti hai. Sasta kaam sasta banda dikhata hai, aur client aage bhi kam hi dega.
Ab pricing ka sahi tareeka. Ek photo ke paise mat socho — packages banao. Log ek se dar jaate hain, package
mein value dikhti hai. Teen tier rakho. Basic — kuch photos, ek product, ek platform ready. Standard — zyada
photos, alag angles, do platform ke sizes. Premium — poora set, lifestyle plus studio, jitne products, sab
platform ready. Har upar wale tier mein zyada value, aur zyada daam.
Numbers ke liye guide — shuru mein Indian sellers ke hisaab se: chhota package sau se hazaar ke beech,
standard hazaar se teen hazaar, premium teen se saat hazaar ke aas-paas. Ye starting hai — jaise portfolio aur
naam banega, daam badhega. Kabhi mehnge shehar ke brand ko chhote daam mat dikhao, aur chhote seller
pe premium mat thopo.
Do zaroori rule. Ek — daam mat girao, scope girao. Client kum budget bole to keemat mat todo, package
chhota kar do — kam photos, kam angles. Isse aapki value bani rehti hai. Do — advance lo. Aadha kaam se
pehle, aadha delivery pe. Bina advance kaam shuru mat karna — ye professional ki nishani hai aur timewaster clients hata deta hai.
Jab client 'kitne loge' puchhe, ghabra ke kuch bhi mat bolo. Confidently package batao, ek line mein value
samjhao — 'is package mein aapko ye ye milega' — aur chup ho jao. Jo pehle bolta hai wo kamzor padta hai.
Ab common galti. Client 'thoda kum karo' bole to log turant aadha kar dete hain. Ye sabse badi galti. Thoda
adjust theek hai, par apni mehnat muft mat karo. Value pe khade raho.
Aaj ka kaam — apna rate card banao. Teen packages, har ek mein kya milega aur kitne ka. Ek page pe saaf
likho, taaki client puchhe to turant bhej sako. 20 minute.

 MODULE 6 — UGC & Brand Deals

LESSON 26 — UGC — Samajh + Pehli Video Banana SCRIPT · ~3min

Aaj se Module 6 shuru — UGC aur brand deals. Aur aaj do cheezein: pehle samjhenge UGC hai kya aur brands
iske itne paise kyun dete hain, phir pehli poori UGC video banayenge. Video ke end tak aapke paas ek ready
UGC video hogi — waisi jaise brands ko chahiye.
UGC matlab User Generated Content. Simple bhasha — aisi video jo aam user ne banayi lage, brand ne nahi.
Jaise koi ladki apne phone se bole 'yaar maine ye face wash try kiya, kamaal hai'. Wo polished ad se zyada
bikti hai, kyunki log ads pe bharosa nahi karte, logon pe karte hain. Isi liye brands crore ka budget chhod ke

LESSON 29 — Deal Close Karna SCRIPT · ~3min

Aaj wo lesson hai jahan mehnat paise mein badalti hai. Brand ka jawab aa gaya — 'haan, interested hain,

itna zyada ki brand bhaag gaya. Dono se bachna hai.
Rate card. Photos ki tarah, UGC bhi packages mein bech. Ek video ka rate mat socho, package socho. Basic —

Payment method aur date likhit mein — ek chhota message hi confirmation hai. Bina advance kaam shuru mat
karo.
Ab common galti. Excitement mein log usage rights, revisions, aur advance ki baat kiye bina 'haan' bol dete

videos, kitni revisions, aur usage. Sab likhit mein.
Aaj ka kaam — apna UGC rate card banao, teen packages ke saath. Phir teen aam sawalon ke jawab likho —
'zyada hai', 'free karke dikhao', 'kitne time ke liye use kar sakte'. Ready rakho taaki brand puchhe to turant
