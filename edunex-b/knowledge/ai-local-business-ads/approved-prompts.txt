Lesson 02 — Business Brief (chat G.P.T, ek hi chat
mein, order se)
Step 1: Details → customers, time, ad angles
I run a small [cafe / salon / bakery / shop] in [area, city]. Details:
- Menu / services: [list]
- Price range: [₹ – ₹]
- Timing: [opening – closing]
- Seating / size: [if any]
- Who usually comes: [students, office people, families...]
- What's special: [only write what is true]
Make a simple business brief:
1. Types of customers and what time of day they come
2. What each type cares about most
3. One ad angle for each type
Keep it short, in simple Hinglish.
Step 2: 5 offers
Suggest 5 offer ideas for this [shop] that are simple and won't hurt profit much — like
combos, a happy hour, or a student deal. For each one, say who it is for and the best day
or time to run it. Don't decide prices — write [price] wherever the owner should fill it
in.

Step 3: 30 din ka post plan
Make a 30-day post plan for this [shop] starting [date]. For each week, tell me what to
post on Instagram and WhatsApp status — a food/product photo, an offer poster, a short
reel, or a behind-the-counter moment. Include any Indian festivals in these dates and mark
them clearly with "(check date)" so I can verify them.
Step 4: Ek page ka Business Brief
Now put everything into one short Business Brief I can save and reuse: business details,
customer types, 3 ad angles, 5 offers, and the 30-day plan. Clean headings, no extra text.
Lesson 03 — Dukaan Ki Photos (Flow · Image · Nano
Banana Pro)
Andar ki photo upgrade
Turn this phone photo of my [shop] into a professional interior photograph, like one shot
by a good photographer for Instagram and Google Maps. Keep the same room, same furniture,
same counter, same boards and same layout — do not add or remove any furniture.
Make these improvements boldly:
- Lighting: replace the harsh tube-light look with warm, soft, inviting light
- Colours: rich and warm, clean walls, natural shine on surfaces
- Camera: straighten the photo, fix the tilt and the stretched wide-angle look, frame it
at normal eye level
- Clean-up: tables wiped clean, chairs neatly pushed in, floor clean, remove clutter,
wires and stains
- Quality: sharp focus, no grain, natural depth, looks like a real DSLR photo
No people, no new decorations, no plants, no text added. Realistic, not AI-looking.
Shop front, shaam ka look
Turn this phone photo of my [shop]'s front into a professional evening photograph. Keep
the same building, same door, same chairs outside and the same signboard — the text "[SHOP
NAME]" must stay exactly the same, do not change, re-write or translate it.
Make these improvements boldly:
- Time: early evening, soft deep-blue sky, warm golden light glowing from inside
- Camera: straighten the photo, fix the tilt, frame the shop front centred and balanced
- Clean-up: remove parked vehicles, wrappers and dust, tidy the overhead wires, clean
footpath
- Colours: rich and warm, sharp focus, no grain, looks like a real DSLR photo
No people, no new signs, no new lights or decorations added. Realistic, not AI-looking.
WhatsApp status version (9:16)
Use this [shop] photo. Make a vertical 9:16 version of the same [shop] for WhatsApp status
— same interior, same furniture, nothing new added. Keep clean empty space in the top
third (a plain warm wall or soft blur) so text can be added later. Do not write any text.

Galat example — dukaan badal deta hai (sirf comparison)
Make my cafe look luxurious and premium.
Lesson 04 — Menu Ki Food Photos
Dish re-shoot
Use this phone photo only as a reference for what our dish looks like. Now re-shoot this
same dish from scratch as a professional food photographer would, for our menu and
Instagram.
The dish: [describe it exactly — e.g. one normal single portion of masala Maggi on a round
steel plate with a steel fork] — exactly the ingredients you see in the reference, nothing
extra.
The new shot:
- Freshly plated, hot and glossy, with light natural steam rising
- Soft warm window light from the left, gentle shadows, rich appetising colours
- 45-degree food angle, close-up, shallow depth of field, sharp focus on the food
- Set on a clean warm wooden table, softly blurred cosy background
Do NOT add cheese, vegetables, coriander, egg or any garnish that is not in the reference.
Same portion size, not bigger. No text, no people. Must look like a real DSLR food photo,
not AI.
Drink re-shoot
Use this phone photo only as a reference for what our drink looks like. Now re-shoot this
same drink from scratch as a professional drink photographer would, for our menu and
Instagram.
The drink: [describe it exactly — e.g. one tall plain glass of cold coffee with a little
foam on top and a straw] — exactly as in the reference, nothing extra.
The new shot:
- Freshly poured, rich colour, light cold condensation drops on the glass
- Soft bright light from behind and the side so the glass glows
- Straight-on eye-level angle, close-up, shallow depth of field
- Set on a clean warm wooden table, softly blurred cosy background
Do NOT add ice cream, whipped cream, syrup, ice on top or any garnish that is not in the
reference. Same glass size, same amount. No text, no people. Must look like a real DSLR
drink photo, not AI.
Combo photo (dono results upload karke)
Use these two photos as reference for the dishes. Re-shoot them together as one
professional "[combo name]" photo: this same [item 1] and this same [item 2] placed side
by side on one clean warm wooden table. Same portions, nothing extra added. Soft warm
window light, 45-degree angle, both sharp, softly blurred background. Leave clean empty
space at the top for text later. Do not write any text. Real DSLR photo, not AI.

Galat example — extra garnish aa jaata hai (sirf comparison)
Make this food look extremely delicious and tempting.
Lesson 05 — Posters
Offer poster
Use this photo of our [items] as the main image. Turn it into a clean, modern offer poster
for Instagram for our [shop]. Keep the food exactly as it is in the photo — do not add or
change anything on the plate or in the glass.
Write ONLY this text, spelled exactly like this, nothing else:
- Top, big bold: "[OFFER NAME]"
- Below it: "[item 1] + [item 2]"
- Big price badge: "₹[price]"
- Small: "[days, time]"
- Bottom: "[SHOP NAME] · [area, city]"
Warm colours, easy-to-read bold fonts, clean layout with the food clearly visible. Do not
add any other words, discounts, percentages, logos or claims.
Tyohaar greeting poster
Use this photo of our shop front as the main image. Make a warm [festival] greeting
poster. Keep the shop, the building and the "[SHOP NAME]" signboard exactly the same — do
not change the shop itself.
Add festive feeling only as a decorative design around the photo: a soft glowing frame of
[diyas / lights / festival elements] along the edges, gentle golden sparkles. Do not place
decorations on the real shop.
Write ONLY this text, spelled exactly like this:
- Top: "[Happy Diwali]"
- Bottom: "Warm wishes from [SHOP NAME]"
Elegant, easy-to-read fonts. No offers, no prices, no other words.

Lesson 06 — Dukaan Ka Video (Flow · Video ·
Ingredients · 9:16 · 6 sec)

Shop front push-in
Use this photo of our shop front as the exact reference. Make a 6-second vertical video:
the camera slowly pushes forward toward the glowing door, smooth and steady, like a calm
evening walk-up. Warm light glows from inside, a gentle breeze, the evening sky slowly
darkens a little. Keep the building, the shutter, the chairs and the "[SHOP NAME]"
signboard exactly the same — the text on the board must not change. Do not add people,
cars, new signs, decorations or lights. Ambient street sound only, no music, no voices, no
text on screen.
Andar glide
Use this photo of our [shop] interior as the exact reference. Make a 6-second vertical
video: the camera slowly glides forward into the [shop] at eye level, as if a customer is
walking in, moving gently toward the counter. Soft warm light, a little steam rising from
the [chai urn / coffee machine]. Keep the same tables, chairs, counter, boards and walls
exactly as in the photo. Do not add people, new furniture, plants, decorations or any
objects that are not in the photo. Soft ambient sound only, no music, no voices, no text
on screen.
Food close-up
Use this photo of our [dish] as the exact reference. Make a 6-second vertical close-up
video: the camera slowly circles a little around the plate, warm natural steam rising,
soft window light. Keep the same plate and the same portion — do not add any other food or
garnish. No hands, no people. Gentle ambient sound only, no music, no voices, no text on
screen.

Lesson 07 — Hindi Voiceover Reel
Step 1: 3 lines (chat G.P.T)
I'm making a 24-second Instagram reel for my [shop] [SHOP NAME] in [area, city]. It has 3
clips of 8 seconds each:
1) the shop front
2) inside the [shop]
3) a close-up of our [best item]
Write one short Hindi voiceover line for each clip, in Devanagari script.
Rules:
- Each line must be easy to say in 6 seconds — maximum 12 words
- Friendly and natural, like talking to a friend
- No claims like "best", "No.1", "sabse sasta" or "famous"
- Use only these facts: [menu, offer with price and timing, opening hours]

Step 2: Awaaz wali clip (Flow · Video · 8 sec · 9:16) — har clip ke liye line badlo
Use this photo of our [shop front / interior / dish] as the exact reference. Make an
8-second vertical video: [one slow camera move — push toward the door / glide inside /
circle the plate]. Keep everything exactly as in the photo — the "[SHOP NAME]" signboard
text must not change. No people on screen.
Voiceover: a warm, friendly young Indian [male / female] narrator, off-screen, speaking
clear natural Hindi at a relaxed pace. Nobody on screen speaks.
TIMING: 0–1s: ambient sound only. 1–7s: the narrator says: "[LINE from chat G.P.T, in
Devanagari]" 7–8s: ambient sound only.
Soft ambient sound under the voice, no music, no text on screen.
Lesson 08 — Posting (chat G.P.T)
Instagram caption + hashtags
Write an Instagram caption for our 24-second reel (shop front → inside → [best item]
close-up). Keep it short and friendly in Hinglish. Mention [area, city] and [offer with
price and timing]. Add 8–10 hashtags — mostly local ones ([city], [area], [type of shop])
and a few [food / service] ones. No claims like "best" or "No.1".
WhatsApp status + broadcast
Now write for WhatsApp:
1) 3 short status lines for this week — one for the reel, one for the offer poster, one
for the festival greeting. Max 10 words each.
2) One short broadcast message for regular customers who have saved our number — friendly,
no pressure, with our timing [opening – closing].
No fake urgency like "only today" or "last chance" unless it is true.
Google Business Profile
Now help with our Google Business Profile:
1) A short "Offer" post for [offer] (max 50 words)
2) Which photos to upload (shop front, inside, food/products) and in what order
3) A polite reply template for a good review, and one for a bad review
Don't write any reviews for us — only the replies.
Hafte ka posting plan
Make a simple weekly posting plan as a table: Day | Instagram | WhatsApp Status | Google
Business. Use the customer timings from our brief to suggest a posting time for each. Keep
it simple enough for one person to manage in about 15 minutes a day.
Lesson 09 — Service (chat G.P.T)

3 packages
I want to offer AI-made marketing content as a service to small local shops in my area —
cafes, salons, bakeries, kirana stores. I only use ChatGPT, Google Flow and CapCut.
Suggest 3 simple monthly packages: Basic, Standard and Plus. For each, list exactly what
the shop gets (for example: number of photo upgrades, offer posters, festival posters,
reels, Google Business posts per month) and roughly how many hours it takes me.
Don't suggest any prices — write [price] where I should fill it in. No promises about
sales, followers or results.
Pehla WhatsApp message
Write a short, polite first WhatsApp message in simple Hinglish that I can send to a local
shop owner I know or have visited. In one line say what I do, offer to make one free
sample poster from a photo of their shop, and say clearly that it's completely okay if
they're not interested. No promises about sales or followers. Maximum 60 words.
Client se sawaal
Make a simple checklist of questions I should ask a shop owner before starting work:
business details, what real photos they can give me, which offers and prices they approve,
festivals they celebrate, which platforms they use, and who will approve posts. Keep it as
a short list I can send on WhatsApp.
"Main kaise kaam karta hoon" note
Write a short "how I work" note I can share with every client, in simple Hinglish: I only
use their real photos and their real prices, every poster and caption is sent to them for
approval before posting, they own all the content I make for them, and how changes work.
Maximum 120 words.
