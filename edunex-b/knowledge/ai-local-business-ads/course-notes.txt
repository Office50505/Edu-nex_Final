AI Local Business Ads
Course Notes
Poore course ke notes — har lesson mein kya seekha, steps, galtiyan aur aaj ka
kaam. Example dukaan: Chai Kona, Vijay Nagar, Indore (sample cafe, demo ke liye).
Course ka poora raasta
Business Brief → dukaan ki photos → menu/food photos → offer aur tyohaar posters → dukaan
ka video → Hindi awaaz wali reel → kahan/kab post karein → ye kaam service ki tarah → final
project.
Tools: chat G.P.T (brief, offers, plan, captions, script), Google Flow (Nano Banana Pro
images, Video), CapCut (clips jodna), ElevenLabs (optional awaaz).
Course ka golden rule: AI se sudhaaro, badlo mat. Asli photo, asli daam, aur har post
maalik ki manzoori se.
Lesson 01 — Course Intro
•Course mein kya banega: business brief, dukaan ki professional photos, menu/food photos,
offer aur tyohaar ke posters, dukaan ka video, Hindi awaaz wali reel, posting plan, aur ye
kaam service ki tarah karna.
•Running example: Vijay Nagar, Indore ka ek chhota cafe — Chai Kona (sab sample details,
demo ke liye).
•Tools: Google Flow, chat G.P.T, CapCut (ElevenLabs optional).
Aaj ka kaam
Ek dukaan chuno — apni ya jaan-pehchaan ki — jis pe poora course follow karoge.
Lesson 02 — chat G.P.T Se Business Brief
•Business Brief is course ki neev hai — aage har poster, reel aur plan isi se banta hai.
•Ek hi chat mein 4 steps: (1) dukaan ki details → customers, unka time, har ek ka ad angle;
(2) 5 offers, daam ki jagah [price]; (3) 30 din ka post plan, tyohaaron pe "(check date)"; (4)
sab ek short brief mein.
•Recording mein chat G.P.T ne khud likha: "Timings estimated hain", aur jo special baat
confirm nahi thi use "Not confirmed" likha.
•Start date nahi di toh chat G.P.T ne date picker se date poochi.
Galti + fix
Shehar aur ilaaka nahi bataya → ad kisi bhi shehar ka lagne lagta hai. Fix: prompt mein saaf
likho kya badalna hai aur kya bilkul nahi badalna.

Honest limitation
Tyohaaron ki tareekhein chat G.P.T galat bhi bata sakta hai — calendar se ek baar milao.
Daam hamesha maalik tay kare.
Aaj ka kaam
Apni chuni dukaan ka brief banao.
Lesson 03 — Dukaan Ki Phone Photo → Professional
•Flow → Image → Nano Banana Pro. Andar ki photo aur shop front ki photo upload karo.
•Prompt mein saaf likho kya-kya behtar karna hai (light, rang, angle, safai) — aur kya nahi
badalna (furniture, layout, board ka naam).
•Shop front ko shaam ka look, andar ki photo warm aur saaf. Phir WhatsApp status ke liye
9:16 version, upar khaali jagah ke saath.
•Ratio wahi rakho jo photo ka hai — warna AI khaali jagah khud bhar deta hai (jaise ceiling
lambi kar dena).
Galti + fix
"Luxurious aur premium bana do" likha → jhoomar, lakdi ki paneling, velvet kursiyan aa gayi.
Ye dukaan hai hi nahi. Fix: prompt mein saaf likho kya badalna hai aur kya bilkul nahi
badalna.
Honest limitation
Camera ka tedhapan har baar theek nahi hota — ek-do baar aur generate karo. Sample
photo use kari ho toh video mein bata do.
Aaj ka kaam
Apni dukaan ki do photos upgrade karo.
Lesson 04 — Menu Ki Food Photos
•Purani photo ko sirf reference banao aur Flow se dish dobara "shoot" karwao — warna AI
wahi photo naye table pe chipka deta hai.
•Dish ko shabdon mein exact likho (plate, portion, kya-kya hai). Phir light, angle, steam,
background.
•Do items ki photo banake dono ko saath daalo → combo photo, upar text ke liye jagah.
•Rule: jo plate grahak ko milti hai, photo mein wahi.
Galti + fix
"Extremely delicious and tempting" likha → dhaniya, mirch aur upar se masala aa gaya, jo
plate mein milta hi nahi. Fix: prompt mein saaf likho kya badalna hai aur kya bilkul nahi
badalna.
Honest limitation
Combo mein items ka size thoda badal sakta hai — 1–2 baar aur generate karke sahi wala
chuno.

Aaj ka kaam
Apne menu ki teen photos banao.
Lesson 05 — Offer Aur Tyohaar Ke Posters
•Combo photo se offer poster: poster pe exact shabd khud likh ke do — "Write ONLY this
text, spelled exactly".
•Shop front se tyohaar ka greeting poster: diye sirf frame/design mein, asli dukaan pe nahi.
•Greeting poster pe offer zaroori nahi — naam aur shubhkamna kaafi.
•Example price (₹99) sirf demo hai — asli daam maalik tay kare.
Galti + fix
"Diwali offer poster bana do" likha → AI ne khud "BUY 1 GET 1 FREE", "Valid Nov 10–15" aur
samosa daal diya, jo menu mein hai hi nahi. Fix: prompt mein saaf likho kya badalna hai aur
kya bilkul nahi badalna.
Honest limitation
Chhota text (timing) kabhi bahut chhota ya galat aata hai. Post se pehle har shabd aur
keemat padho.
Aaj ka kaam
Ek offer aur ek tyohaar poster banao.
Lesson 06 — Dukaan Ki Photos Se Video
•Flow → Video → Ingredients → 9:16 → 6 sec. Ek photo, ek dheema camera movement.
•Teen clips: shop front (darwaze ki taraf push), andar (counter ki taraf glide), food (plate ke
around halka circle).
•Prompt mein likho: board ka text same, koi naya insaan/furniture nahi.
•Teeno clips CapCut mein jodo → 18 sec ki sachchi reel.
Galti + fix
"Cinematic video of my cafe" likha → menu pe "MAFENU" aur ₹350–450 ke daam aa gaye,
naye grinder aa gaye — dukaan hi badal gayi. Fix: prompt mein saaf likho kya badalna hai
aur kya bilkul nahi badalna.
Honest limitation
Video mein board ya menu ka text bigad sakta hai — har clip dhyan se dekho.
Aaj ka kaam
Dukaan ki teen clip wali reel banao.
Lesson 07 — Hindi Voiceover Wali Reel
•chat G.P.T se 3 lines (har clip ke liye ek): max 12 shabd, koi "best/No.1" nahi, sirf diye gaye
facts.

•Recording wali lines: "Vijay Nagar mein Chai Kona, chalo kuch khaate-peete hain!" · "Chai,
cold coffee, sandwich aur fries, jo mann kare mangao!" · "Maggi aur cold coffee, student
combo sirf ninyanve rupaye!"
•Flow Video (8 sec, 9:16): prompt mein Voiceover wala hissa + timing + line. Awaaz ka
description teeno clips mein same copy karo.
•Dusra tareeka: ElevenLabs (Text to Speech) se awaaz banao aur L06 ke silent videos pe
CapCut mein lagao.
Honest limitation
Teeno clips mein awaaz thodi alag lag sakti hai — sun ke check karo, alag lage toh wahi clip
dobara.
Aaj ka kaam
Apni reel pe Hindi awaaz daalo.
Lesson 08 — Kahan Aur Kab Post Karein
•Teen jagah: Instagram (caption + local hashtags), WhatsApp (status + sirf saved number
walon ko broadcast), Google Business Profile (offer post, photos ka order, review replies).
•Weekly plan: din, platform, time — customers ke time ke hisaab se. Daily 15 minute ki
checklist.
•chat G.P.T ne Google Business ke liye dusre cafes ki example photos dikhayi aur khud likha
"Upload real photos" — hamesha apni asli photos.
Honest limitation
Posting time sirf andaaza hai, data nahi. 2–3 hafte baad dekho kya chala aur badlo. Nakli
review ya khareede followers kabhi nahi.
Aaj ka kaam
Apni dukaan ka hafte ka post plan banao.
Lesson 09 — Ye Kaam Service Ki Tarah
•chat G.P.T se 3 packages (Basic, Standard, Plus): kya milega + kitne ghante lagenge.
Daam ki jagah [price] — apne shehar ke hisaab se khud tay karo.
•Pehla WhatsApp message: chhota, ek free sample poster ka offer, aur "interested nahi ho
toh koi baat nahi".
•Kaam se pehle client se sawaal: details, asli photos, approved offers/daam, tyohaar,
platforms, approval kaun dega.
•"Main kaise kaam karta hoon" note: asli photos, asli daam, har post maalik ki manzoori se,
content ka maalik client, ek round changes.
Honest limitation
Sample bhejo toh saaf batao ki demo hai. "Bikri dugni" jaise waade kabhi nahi.
Aaj ka kaam
Paas ki ek dukaan ke liye sample banao.

Lesson 10 — Finale: Galtiyan, Niyam, Final Project
•Recap: brief → photos → food → posters → video → awaaz → posting plan → service.
•Top 3 galtiyan: (1) adhoora prompt (jhoomar, dhaniya), (2) bina padhe post karna
(MAFENU, check date), (3) AI ko khud offer banane dena (Buy 1 Get 1).
•Teen niyam: asli photo, asli daam, har post maalik ki manzoori se.
•Saare prompts notes section mein hain.
Aaj ka kaam
Final project: ek asli dukaan (maalik se poochh ke) ka poora set — brief, 2 photo upgrade, 1
food/product photo, 1 offer poster, 1 awaaz wali reel, hafte ka plan — aur maalik ko dikhao.
