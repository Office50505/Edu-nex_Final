Skillomate provides paid AI video courses, learning progress, offline lessons and Nex AI tutoring.

GUEST ACCESS (no account required)
Launch the app without signing in. If the sign-in screen is shown, choose "Continue as Guest". Browse Home/Courses, open a course, and view its description and lesson list without supplying personal information. Paid lessons remain locked for guests; signing in alone does not grant membership.

ACCOUNT AND PAID FEATURES
Use the review account supplied in the Sign-In Information fields to test protected membership features. With an active entitlement, open a course and play a lesson to review playback and saved progress. Profile, wishlist, downloads, certificates and account controls use the signed-in account. The review credentials are provided separately, not in these notes.

APPLE IN-APP PURCHASE
The membership screen includes the Apple subscription offer, localized price/renewal terms, "Restore Purchases" and "Refresh Access". Restore uses verified Apple entitlements. Existing eligible members can sign in and refresh access. Apple subscriptions are managed through the App Store subscription-management link. Privacy and terms are available from the membership screen.

OPTIONAL AI DATA SHARING
In Nex AI or lesson Course AI, the first send checks the current data-sharing choice. Before external AI processing, the app identifies the AI providers and shared information and offers "Allow AI", "Not now" and the privacy-policy link. Declining leaves course access available. Manage or withdraw permission under Nex AI > Chat history > AI Data Controls > AI data sharing permission. Delete AI history is available separately there. Profile, contact, demographic, membership and payment records are not automatically attached to external AI requests; personal information users type into messages can still be shared.

ACCOUNT DELETION
Profile > Delete Account offers in-app deletion after password verification and typed DELETE confirmation. Apple-billed subscriptions are managed separately; the app provides the subscription-management link and warning.

AGE AND PRIVACY
The age rating uses the 13+ override to match the minimum-age terms, with Apple's regional equivalents. The App Store privacy answers include account-linked optional profile-photo uploads, age/gender data, learning activity, AI/user content and support diagnostics. Optional profile photos use the system picker.

REVIEW SUPPORT
Please use the App Review contact information if assistance is needed. The requested physical-device recording must be supplied in the review attachment/link area before resubmission.

PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
Skillomate is an education service for learners aged 13 and above who want structured, practical instruction in AI filmmaking, content creation and related tools. It combines course videos/materials, saved learning progress and optional course-aware tutoring. It does not promise employment or income results.

EXTERNAL SERVICES
Core infrastructure uses AWS hosting, S3/CloudFront media delivery and MongoDB storage. Phone authentication uses MSG91. iOS digital membership uses Apple In-App Purchase and App Store transaction/status verification. Nex AI uses fal.ai/OpenRouter and the configured model provider identified in the permission notice (Google Gemini by default). Separate web/Android billing integrations are not substitutes for the iOS StoreKit purchase flow.

REGIONAL INFORMATION
The published terms identify India as the intended service market, and account registration uses Indian phone numbers. StoreKit prices, offer eligibility, currency and renewal terms depend on the App Store account/storefront and are displayed by Apple. The review account supplied separately supports review of account-based features.

CONTENT RIGHTS AND REGULATED FEATURES
This is an education service, with no medical diagnosis, regulated financial trading or gambling functionality identified. App Store Connect records the developer's declaration of necessary third-party content rights. Any applicable authorization/licence evidence requested for protected course or media assets must accompany the final submission.

IN-APP PURCHASE OVERVIEW
The Skillomate Premium Monthly subscription provides eligible paid membership access. Navigate to the membership screen from a locked paid lesson or the profile membership controls. The app shows the applicable App Store price and terms, purchase, Restore Purchases, Refresh Access and subscription-management controls.
